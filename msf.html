MSF presents itself as an intelligence service. Its founder built a second purpose beneath that public mission, preparing an organization of mostly ordinary people to approach threats they were never told existed.

01

Dual mandate

What members see depends on where they stand

PUBLIC FUNCTION

Intelligence service

Officers investigate threats, collect information, and conduct operations through methods that appear entirely political and terrestrial.

FOUNDER'S DESIGN

Contingency structure

Fionn quietly shaped MSF so it could confront magical crises. Most members were not told that this purpose, or magic itself, existed.

STORY PRESSURE

Forced convergence

The Spill gradually makes the two missions impossible to separate. Intelligence work brings ordinary officers into direct contact with a hidden world.

02

Command succession

Every director inherits a different agency

  1. 01
    FionnFounder and concealed Magiarch

    Builds MSF's public intelligence capacity while concealing its deeper purpose from most of the organization.

  2. 02
    HelenaSeizure and redirection

    Takes control after Fionn's elimination and frames Lynleit for his death, turning succession into a weapon.

  3. 03
    LynleitRestoration and contraction

    Fights to clear her name and reclaim the agency. MSF later becomes smaller and increasingly focused on containing the Spill.

  4. 04
    KyrienNon-Magus director

    Inherits the organization after Lynleit disappears and survives by fully developing MSF's intelligence, evasion, and operational side.

03

Political footprint

Turon contains more than a headquarters

MSF's concentration of officers in Turon is sufficient to make a small rebellion or secession imaginable.

No open threat has been made. The possibility remains in the backrooms of political life, where institutional capacity matters even when nobody intends to use it.
