MSF

Private intelligence contractor

One agency,
two purposes

MSF is a fully private intelligence business that sells its services to the government. The initials are used publicly without a formal expansion. Fionn built a second purpose beneath its contracted work, preparing mostly ordinary people to approach threats they were never told existed.

01

Dual mandate

What members see depends on where they stand

CONTRACTED FUNCTION

Government services

MSF sells intelligence, investigation, security, and operational services to the government while remaining institutionally private.

FOUNDER'S DESIGN

Contingency structure

Fionn quietly shaped MSF so it could confront magical crises. Most members were not told that this purpose, or magic itself, existed.

PERMANENT CONVERGENCE

Two threat domains

The Spill is never fully contained. MSF must shrink into a mainly Magi-focused task force while continuing to handle the ordinary intelligence threats it was created to confront.

Father Mikhail's discovery that Lynleit has disclosed magic to Kyrien, Felix, and Reiner forces the first institutional answer to knowledge already shared. Lynleit refuses to surrender the three witnesses and instead begins moving MSF toward a partly Magi private enterprise accountable to the Magiarchy and the Church.

02

Ownership and control

The government buys access, not ownership

PRIVATE GOVERNANCE

Its client cannot run the company

The government can negotiate contracts, purchase defined services, and decide whether to keep buying them. It has little authority over MSF's succession, command culture, internal appointments, or ordinary workings beyond the access MSF chooses to permit. MSF may also accept clients beyond the government, exposing the danger of a private contract whose obligations conflict with Narvea's interests.

POLITICAL NICKNAME
“A legal mafia.”

Politically savvy citizens use the joke because MSF combines private loyalty, public contracts, internal succession, and enough concentrated force to make the state uneasy, all while operating within the law.

Cryptology in plain sight

Authority is worn in blue

MSF · B01
ENCRYPTED M.O.

A visible signal that looks ordinary

Fionn built cryptology into everyday appearance. A deliberate blue garment initiates recognition, but it does not authenticate authority by itself. Top officers follow it with an innocuous challenge-response hidden inside ordinary conversation.

Anyone can buy a blue coat. Only someone trusted by Fionn knows how to answer a question that sounds like small talk.

04

Command succession

Fionn protects every heir by naming none

MSF is a private family enterprise, and its leaders settle succession internally. Fionn deliberately refuses to identify one uncontested successor because he believes a declared heir would become an obvious target. His death divides the inheritance: Lynleit eventually becomes the new Magiarch, while Kyrien inherits the MSF director's seat during her absence. The government has little standing to settle the vacancy, and Fionn's security measure produces the succession crisis it was intended to prevent.

  1. 01
    FionnFounder without a declared heir

    Distributes trust through private protocols and overlapping circles, but refuses to turn any one candidate into a visible target.

  2. 02
    HelenaContested inheritance

    Takes control after Fionn's elimination and frames Lynleit for his death. Her ignorance of his private authority protocol gradually gives influential officers reason to doubt the legitimacy of her succession.

  3. 03
    LynleitRestoration and contraction

    Fights to clear her name and reclaim the agency. Because the Spill cannot be fully contained, she reshapes MSF into a smaller task force centered on Magi while preserving its intelligence function.

  4. 04
    KyrienNon-Magus director in the Magiarch's absence

    Inherits control while Lynleit carries the Magiarchate. He must keep a private company, its government contracts, and its permanent Spill mandate functioning without magical authority or the founder's declared succession.

05

Political footprint

MSF's concentration of force in Turon

MSF's concentration of officers in Turon is sufficient to make a small rebellion or secession imaginable.

Officer culture, divided logistics, constitutional loyalty, and the absence of any desire to rebel keep that capacity dormant. Politicians remain uneasy because the state cannot simply command or reorganize its private contractor. The final safeguard is the belief that MSF officers are decent people.
