World/The Director's House

Residence · family seat · operational node

A family home
with institutional gravity

The three-floor house is both the director's residence and the family's long-term home. Its domestic life never fully separates from MSF: offices, a library, and discreet working rooms allow it to function as a small command post without becoming a formal headquarters.

Behind the house, a compact private park descends toward the river-facing edge of the hill. The elevation gives the upper floor a long view across the city while keeping the garden closely tied to the first-floor living spaces.

03Highest floor

Lynleit's chosen room

A desk above the city

During her school years, Lynleit specifically asked for a bedroom on the third floor. She wanted enough height to look into the distance while doing homework, turning the room's view into part of her private routine long before leadership made that habit feel symbolic.

02Private interior

Family domain

The quieter center of the house

The second floor belongs to the residence's private interior. Its precise room plan is not yet fixed, preserving space for family rooms and the boundaries between household life and the work conducted elsewhere in the building.

01Garden level

Glass and greenery

Nature without surrendering shelter

The rear side of the first floor opens into glass-enclosed relaxation areas facing the garden. In summer they extend domestic life into the greenery. In winter the same rooms preserve the view and atmosphere while protecting everyone inside from cold, rain, and wind.

A substantial domestic library supports study, research, and the quieter habits of a family whose private responsibilities extend beyond the household.

Multiple offices allow private meetings and administrative work to occur without converting the entire residence into an overt agency building.

03

Dual role

Residence and command space occupy the same address

FAMILY NEST

A place that belongs to the family first

Bedrooms, study, reading, the garden, and the glass relaxation rooms give the house a genuine domestic identity. It stores ordinary memories alongside the pressures attached to Fionn's position.

SHARED THRESHOLDPrivacy becomes operational access

The director can receive trusted people, review sensitive work, and continue agency business without leaving the protection and familiarity of home.

MINI-HQ

A discreet extension of MSF

The offices and library let the residence support selected meetings, planning, and confidential work. It complements MSF's formal headquarters in Turon rather than replacing it.
