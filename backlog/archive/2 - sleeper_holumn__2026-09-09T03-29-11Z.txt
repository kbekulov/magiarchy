CORE HOLUMN CONCEPT

The Sleepers appear as human figures suspended high in the air, usually approximately twenty to fifty metres above the ground or water.

They resemble people hanging from invisible puppet strings.

Their heads are lowered as though asleep, unconscious, or dead.

Their arms and legs hang loosely.

They may move slightly with the wind.

Their clothing may move naturally.

They remain otherwise still.

They do not attack witnesses.

They do not chase them.

They do not communicate.

They do not visibly react to being watched.

Their presence is ominous despite their complete passivity.

Most of the figures in Kyrien's scene appear to be men wearing trench coats and hats.

Do not establish whether they are actual human beings.
