we currently have a H Scene toggle for panels, but I think we need to permeate this across the entire story lore, as in, not only panels can have H Scenes, but also chapters, moments, holumns, and whatever else you consider could accomodate H Scenes. They should though all have a special indicator to them implying danger, both for the writer and reader. I'll leave it up to you to decide how to imply that visual warning in a tasteful way while still sticking true to the core philosophy of our UI/UX site look. 
H Scenes can also be differentiated between unnecessary ones (which the author created just for character exploration but they don't serve the story as much as they should. And then H Scenes which are directly entrenched into the story as an important episode, but they may be either explicitly described, or indirectly implied. (I suppose your visual warning system can have 2 layers: explicit +/- important).
As a rule, whenever I share a H scene material of any kind with you directly or via backlog, always ask me whether its content should be treated as side-content, with no impact on canon characters and lore, or if it should be treated as integration into the lore, which implies the events occurring in the story might reshape characters and their relationships. You can of course immediately identify H scene related content if anything sexual or romantic is involved in it.
