### Sherie may genuinely not know whether she is being seductive

I would not make this a calculated “seduction pose.”

That makes her less interesting.

She is aristocratic enough that sprawling elegantly across a huge sofa, stockings visible, skirt settling high on the thighs, one hand at her temple and legs stretched across the cushions could genuinely feel normal to her.

She grew up in an environment where bodily composure, beautiful clothes, elegant furniture and controlled presentation are simply part of existence.

So initially, in her own head:

I’m comfortable.

Felix’s brain:

She is lying six feet away from me looking like THAT.

And Sherie may genuinely need several seconds before she realizes there is a discrepancy.

That is where her emotional perceptiveness becomes dangerous.

She notices Felix stop talking for half a second.

Her eyes flick toward him.

He continues.

She notices his gaze drop and return.

Now she knows.

And here is the important distinction:

She did not necessarily adopt the pose to seduce him.

But once she realizes the pose is affecting him...

That is where the seduction begins.

Maybe she stretches one leg another few centimeters.

Maybe her crossed ankles uncross.

Maybe she shifts deeper into the cushion.

Nothing blatant enough to acknowledge.

Teehee.
