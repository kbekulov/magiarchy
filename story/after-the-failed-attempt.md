## The Interview Room

Kyrien's attempt on a politician has failed for a reason he could not have anticipated: the target belongs to the hidden world of Magi. He now faces prison, and the police office gives him no reason to expect another outcome.

Lynleit enters the interrogation with authority that has not yet been explained. She does not offer forgiveness. She offers conditional freedom.

Kyrien may avoid prison if he becomes her secret helper. The arrangement leaves him confined to her demands, useful precisely because Helena does not know he belongs to Lynleit's circle.

## The Warning

Lynleit cannot prove that a crisis is coming. Her suspicion of Helena is built from a tense family history, things she has noticed but not yet explained, and an intuition that tells her the present balance will not hold.

Recruiting Kyrien is a high-risk answer to a danger she cannot name. Fionn's assassination is still far away. Lynleit does not foresee the event itself, but she acts with the conviction that she will soon need an ally her enemies cannot anticipate.

## The Compartment

Lynleit later tells only Felix and Reiner. Even Fionn remains uninformed.

Both friends see Kyrien as a possible danger to her. Reiner accepts the arrangement because he trusts Lynleit's judgment. Felix yields because Reiner's decision leaves him in the minority, but his distrust of Kyrien remains.

The wager stays hidden. Much later, when Fionn is assassinated and Helena moves against Lynleit, the danger she sensed finally takes visible form.
