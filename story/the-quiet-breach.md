# Chapter 01: The Quiet Breach

## The Call

The breach looked harmless on the first report: one locked room, one missing witness, and a pressure reading too small to justify an MSF response. Lynleit went anyway.

Kyrien was already waiting across the street. He told her the numbers were clean because someone had cleaned them.

## The Choice

Inside, every clock showed a different time. Fionn's voice came through Lynleit's receiver, calm and distant, ordering her to contain the room and leave the cause untouched.

Kyrien found a second door where the wall should have been. Opening it would break protocol. Leaving it closed would preserve a lie.

Lynleit reached for the handle.

## End Record

The room vanished before the containment team arrived. Only one clock remained, still counting backward.
