# Episode 03: The Name Left Behind

> Placeholder draft. Replace this episode when final story material is available.

## Missing Entry

Natalia noticed the gap because the archive insisted there was none. A page number had been removed from every index, leaving the sequence perfect and the history incomplete.

The missing name led her to the Magic Academy, where Myka had hidden the last unaltered copy inside a student spellbook.

## The Witness

Fionn's name appeared in the margin beside a date that had not happened yet. Myka refused to explain how the record had been written.

Someone entered the archive below them. The shelves began correcting themselves one book at a time.

## End Record

Natalia photographed the page before it turned blank. The image preserved every word except the name she had come to find.
