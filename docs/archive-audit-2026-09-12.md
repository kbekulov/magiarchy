# Archive Audit: 12 September 2026

## Status and scope

**Findings and proposals only. The author has not approved implementation of the actions below.** Today's character clarifications have been integrated separately in the [Cognition and Physical Competence reference](../docs.html?doc=character-cognition-and-physical-competence). That integration does not authorize the revisions proposed here.

Jump to [consistency and chronology](#first-priority-factual-consistency-and-chronology), [story and writing](#story-prose-and-reader-comprehension), [UI and reading](#ui-and-reading-experience), [writer workflow](#writer-workflow-and-cohesion), or [recommended order](#recommended-order-after-approval).

The review covered the current public pages, 18 profiles, 8 default Chapters, 24 current Moments, 14 registered documents, World records, shared registries, editorial ledgers, search, navigation, and version handling. Current narrative text received the closest editorial review. Historical revisions were checked for separation and sampled for context, not treated as current canon or exhaustively line-edited. Original artwork and audio were not altered.

Automated browser checks exercised 80 routes at 390, 1024, and 1440 pixels in Chromium and WebKit: 480 views. They found no broken displayed images, page-wide horizontal overflow, or browser script errors. They did find two local text-overflow problems at 1024 pixels. Existing regression checks also covered navigation, search history, portrait thumbnails, relationship selection, and masonry hover stability. These are useful checks, not proof of complete accessibility or of behavior on a physical iPhone. Native Safari, VoiceOver, and live authenticated Page notes were not exercised by the local sweep.

The Nasu audit was applied as the project's advisory framework, not as participation by Kinoko Nasu. Its principal influence is to require observable preparation, consequential limits, and earned exceptions without explaining away the mysteries. The author's established outcomes remain controlling.

## What is already working

- Natalia's worked examples give complex material a human scale. Her familiar teacher-student rhythm with Lynleit is worth preserving.
- The river's empty boats, submerged hands, lost jacket, and divided banks provide specific action and imagery rather than interchangeable supernatural danger.
- The Nameless Street has a strong sequence of objects and actions: balloon, crying, fence, body, smile, fall. Its uncertainty is productive.
- Mikhail and Lynleit's prolonged exchange has recognizable comic business and escalation. Length alone is not a reason to dismantle it.
- Sherie and Felix now have a relationship direction distinct from Lynleit and Kyrien. The difference should survive scene drafting.
- Shared registries, version snapshots, ordinary-looking entity links, flat styling, and the mobile archive toolbars provide a sound base. A wholesale site redesign is not justified by this audit.

## First priority: factual consistency and chronology

### R01. Lester's foundation entry has not caught up with his ontology

**Evidence:** [Hidden World Foundation](../docs.html?doc=world-foundation) still leaves open whether Lester was born a demiholumn or became one. His [profile](../character.html?character=lester) and the [Holumn archive](../holumns.html) expressly reject an ordinary discoverable transformation as the explanation. The older wording reopens a route the author closed.

**Proposed action:** Version and update the foundation document to preserve intrinsic contradiction, the limits of human comprehension, and Natalia's recognition. Keep questions about his capabilities and observable biography open. Do not invent an origin event.

**Decision needed:** Implementation approval only; the governing author decision already exists.

### R02. The Drowned Choir's stated boundary is broader than the scene supports

**Evidence:** HI-003's countermeasure summary says the hands cannot reach through the river surface. Maren appears to be pulled from the boat before she is in the water, but the blocking does not explain the first grip under that broad restriction. The incident's fuller description instead locates the impassable boundary beneath water-walking Lynleit. HI-003 also says the dead speak through the girl, although the encounter presents her as silent.

**Proposed action:** Distinguish the observed boundary under Lynleit from a universal restriction on the hands. Check the source of the claim about speaking before retaining it as fact. Preserve the Choir and leviathan as separate Holumns.

**Decision needed:** Approval to reconcile the wording; author confirmation if the boundary is meant to have a broader mechanism or the Choir has an established voice outside this encounter.

### R03. The interrogation outline contains stale exposition and repeated entry

**Evidence:** [After the Failed Attempt](../story.html?chapter=after-the-failed-attempt) introduces Lynleit's entrance twice in successive paragraphs and says her authority has not been explained. Her profile already names the MSF transfer clause. An explanation deliberately withheld from Kyrien is different from an unresolved explanation in the archive.

**Proposed action:** Remove the repeated entrance and align the outline's account of what is known with the owning records. Preserve the protected politician, the danger to Lynleit, her concealed knowledge, and the coercive initial arrangement. Do not invent interrogation dialogue merely to fill the gap.

**Decision needed:** Implementation approval only for the consistency edit; drafting the full encounter is a separate action.

### R04. Some reciprocal or absolute claims need a source decision

**Evidence:** The World index says Natalia was Lynleit's favourite teacher. Natalia's profile and the Academy Moment additionally call Lynleit Natalia's favourite student. Those are separate claims. Kyrien's later intelligence work is described as mastered completely, which exceeds a bounded account of competence.

**Proposed action:** Confirm whether the favourite-student relationship is reciprocal. Replace unsupported absolutes with the specific work a character can accomplish, without reducing established competence. Apply the same source check to current records rather than treating every old sentence as an author decision.

**Decision needed:** Confirm Natalia's preference if it is not already in an authoritative source. No new weaknesses should be invented to balance the wording.

### R05. Approximate chronology still looks more fixed than it is

**Evidence:** Doom's approximate phase appears in the single ordered timeline after the political settlement. The text says its relation to surrounding phases is unfixed, but the visual order suggests otherwise. The flask's unknown origin receives an Arc 0 chronology entry labelled Family history even though its source and beginning remain open. The Nameless Street shares the Cardiff/Academy phase without establishing its exact relation to the journeys.

**Proposed action:** Display approximate ranges, concurrent strands, and genuinely unplaced entries without forcing a single exact sequence. Keep the confirmed order of the hotel refuge, Doom, park encounter, later intimacy, pregnancy, and Arc 2. Remove an unearned period assignment from unknown item history; preserve stable links and flexible numbering.

**Decision needed:** Approval for the presentation and metadata work. Exact dates, durations, and the relationship of Doom to political milestones remain author decisions.

### R06. Reveal order needs its own record, distinct from event order

**Evidence:** Profiles, timelines, incident explanations, and Chapter tables contain different levels of knowledge about the first missing person, Fionn's death, the coat, the Cult connection, and Lynleit's children. The reader understanding the son is not the same event as Kyrien finding out. The current Cult naming follows the approved later Doom revisions and should not be silently returned to the earlier implied-only treatment.

**Proposed action:** Track when an event occurs, when the reader can infer or learn it, and when each relevant character knows it. Begin with the small set of major secrets already established. Preserve the author's current revision decisions and leave undetermined revelations blank.

**Decision needed:** Approve the model; choose any still-undecided revelation dates or audiences only when the story supplies them.

## Story, prose, and reader comprehension

### R07. Decide how much the archive should spoil for a first-time reader

**Evidence:** The Story timeline exposes the first missing person's causal role before the Chapters. Preface tables reveal outcomes before the prose, and profiles span later life periods. These are useful writer references but can remove discovery from the reading experience.

**Proposed action:** Keep the required preface tables, but offer a clear reading mode or a collapsed spoiler summary. Keep the complete writer view available. Separate intended chronology from recommended reading order rather than pretending the archive is already a finished novel.

**Decision needed:** Whether this site should support spoiler-conscious reading at all, and whether that or the writer view should be the default. No spoilers have been hidden during this audit.

### R08. Draft status and Chapter coverage need clearer presentation

**Evidence:** The catalog places 141-word and 225-word placeholders beside developed scenes of roughly 1,400 to 3,600 words. The status badges exist, but all entries share sequential Chapter labels and the same reader architecture. The Name Left Behind introduces a future-dated record about Fionn and self-altering archives without a matching Moment. Before the First Bell also lacks a linked Moment.

**Proposed action:** Make outlines, drafted scenes, canon drafts, and writer gaps distinguishable in catalog filtering and reader headers. Add missing Moment links where the material is accepted. Keep the invented archive incident outside shared canon until accepted or explicitly retired; do not delete its historical source.

**Decision needed:** Whether to keep and develop The Name Left Behind, or retain it only as an unused outline. Creating a Moment must not automatically ratify its inventions.

### R09. The main political and supernatural plot needs more scene work

**Evidence:** The central recruitment remains an outline. Fionn's murder, the refuge, reclamation of MSF, the Cardiff moral conflict, and the Academy rescue mostly exist as planning records rather than Chapters. Doom has received substantially more dialogue and revision work than those turning points.

**Proposed action:** Prioritize a small sequence of main-plot scenes before adding further intimate milestones: the interrogation, a professional encounter showing Sherie and Felix, an operational consequence of Kyrien's preparation, and a scene exposing the pressure around Fionn. Draft only where facts are sufficient; request focused decisions for missing causes.

**Decision needed:** Which scene to develop first. This is a proposal for writing work, not permission to manufacture the missing conspiracy or chronology.

### R10. Doom's recovery needs more visible support in the characters

**Evidence:** [Doom v7](../story.html?chapter=doom-has-an-address&version=v7) carefully presents the proposed cure as uncertain, then explains categorically that the incomplete attempt and aftermath changed both identities enough. The particular change in Kyrien is less visible than the explanation of the mechanism. The final assurance risks doing work the preceding behavior should carry.

**Proposed action:** Develop observable, non-graphic changes in attention, choice, or expectation on both sides, and review the transition in narrative viewpoint around the recovery. Preserve the incomplete attempt, the successful recovery, Kyrien's incomplete knowledge, Natalia's practical teaching, and Mikhail's comic exchange. Do not introduce a new ritual, completed sexual encounter, or guaranteed cure formula.

**Decision needed:** Approval for a new revision and, if necessary, agreement on the particular changes each character undergoes. The outcome itself is not being reopened.

### R11. Recheck shown and inferred labels against specific evidence

**Evidence:** The river preface interprets physical tells as attraction and the empty boats as evidence of the crews' fate. These may be intentional readings, but they are not equally certain deductions. Elsewhere explanatory narration makes material explicit that an older outline may have left inferred. The park contains a visible writer gap, which is a different status again.

**Proposed action:** Attach each current table claim to the passage that supports it, soften claims where the evidence supports only a possibility, and distinguish facts supplied by the author from conclusions available inside the scene. Preserve the blue/purple reader key and neutral outline facts. An undrafted passage must not be presented as delivered evidence.

**Decision needed:** Approval for the label and wording audit. Any change to a intended reveal itself needs the author's agreement.

### R12. Revise explanatory habits without flattening the voices

**Evidence:** The interrogation repeats an entrance. Institutional headings repeatedly announce abstractions such as two purposes or three degrees of distance before explaining the actual subject. Some profile claims are broad verdicts. Several scenes use the same short denial, correction, and counter-correction rhythm, which can make different speakers converge.

**Proposed action:** Perform a passage-level edit for repetition, focalization, abstract summary, and unnecessary justification. Keep productive comic repetition, prop business, domestic detail, and the author's approved extended banter. Distinguish Felix's generated alternatives from Hiyu's investigative hypotheses, Kyrien's observation from Reiner's procedural speech, and Lynleit's familiar irreverence from Sherie's social play. Use today's behavior as attention, not dialogue explaining a type.

**Decision needed:** Approval for selected current documents and Chapters, preserving old published revisions. No imitation of another writer's prose is required.

### R13. The capability graphs still imply false precision and comparison

**Evidence:** The [Capability ledger](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance) already records Lester's Magecraft score despite his inherent demiholumn nature, Mikhail's missing counter-Magus distinction, and the mixing of Kyrien's later mastery with earlier competence. Exact numbers suggest calibrated comparisons that the canon does not yet support.

**Proposed action:** First distinguish story period and type of capability. Separate trained magecraft, Church countermeasures, and inherent anomalous ability where comparison would otherwise mislead. Use a supported descriptive scale or an explicitly bounded graph model. Keep today's agility, endurance, precision, and trained competence independent of MBTI. Do not simply reduce high scores until shapes look balanced.

**Decision needed:** Agreement on what the graph should compare. Specific training histories, failure limits, and supernatural costs remain open.

### R14. Supporting characters need decisions that belong to them

**Evidence:** Tien is strongly defined by serving Helena and opposing Kyrien; Myka's danger and eventual success have more definition than her own decisions; the Cardiff disagreement identifies moral incompatibility without establishing the act that makes it unavoidable. Broad statements about competence or morality cannot yet carry those scenes.

**Proposed action:** Develop one consequential choice, one dependency, and one credible limit for each character when their next scene is written. For Cardiff, identify the specific solution Mikhail accepts that Lynleit cannot accept. For Myka, establish something she does that affects her survival and later recruitment. Preserve specialist distinctions rather than giving every supporting character the protagonists' skills.

**Decision needed:** Concrete author choices. The audit should not invent Mikhail's ethics, Myka's curriculum, or Tien's private loyalties.

### R15. Relationship direction needs evidence across ordinary work

**Evidence:** Sherie and Felix's confirmed gradual direction is richer than the professional encounters currently drafted. Much intimacy guidance describes anticipated behavior. A hypothetical response to Sherie's hurt is not yet a scene. Lynleit and Kyrien's rescue and secrecy milestones are better documented than their mundane repeated contact.

**Proposed action:** Give future main-plot scenes space for small, specific interactions that accumulate. Track established encounters separately from intended development. Preserve Felix's attachment to Lynleit while Sherie acquires significance of her own, and keep Lynleit's duty primary. Do not add a confession simply to complete an audit checklist.

**Decision needed:** Approval to develop particular encounters, with chronology kept open where it is not fixed.

### R16. Foundational terms need a shorter route into the setting

**Evidence:** Definitions of magic, magecraft, miracles, Holumns, identity, and suppression are distributed across World and Docs. Human singularity appears in Lester's account without a short local explanation of what the phrase permits the reader to understand. Institutional interpretations can sound like universal physical laws when read in isolation.

**Proposed action:** Provide a concise entry point with links to owning definitions. Distinguish observed effects, institutional doctrine, and unanswered ontology. Cross-link existing explanations before adding another large foundational essay. Move public development prompts such as the Magiarchy page's Awaiting definition section into the editorial ledgers, while retaining honest statements about unknown phenomena. Preserve unknown mechanisms rather than filling them with invented technical language.

**Decision needed:** Approval for organization and clearer wording; any new metaphysical definition requires author confirmation.

## UI and reading experience

### R17. Fix the two confirmed intermediate-width overflows

**Evidence:** At 1024 pixels in both engines, the Director's House heading has about 199 pixels of content in a 162-pixel column. The Holumn definition's first explanation exceeds its local column as well. The house keeps a three-column hero until a 950-pixel breakpoint; its fixed side columns and padding leave the title too narrow. The Holumn layout nests another two-column grid inside an already constrained panel.

**Proposed action:** Collapse these compositions according to available content width, reduce unsuitable fixed minimums, and allow long text to wrap. Test the full interval around the breakpoints and browser zoom. Preserve the distinct page designs and flat palette.

**Decision needed:** Implementation approval only. Neither issue was fixed in this audit.

### R18. Restore visible life-period context to profile artwork

**Evidence:** The portrait carousel updates Arc information in image alt text, thumbnail titles, and a screen-reader-only status. A sighted touch user cannot reliably identify the displayed Arc 2 artwork from those labels. Catalog cards do display an Arc label.

**Proposed action:** Add a small visible era label only when relevant, without restoring arrow buttons, image counters, a large overlay background, or obstructed artwork. Keep image-half taps, dragging, swiping, and thumbnails.

**Decision needed:** Implementation approval and placement preference only if the author wants to choose it.

### R19. Remove misleading empty choices and distinguish music concepts

**Evidence:** All 33 current Gallery records use unspecified location, while the location selector still offers several named places with no matching images. Music has one playable recording and four concepts; the category architecture now matches Characters, but concepts still occupy the same prominent browsing space as playable work.

**Proposed action:** Derive available filter choices and counts from records, without inventing locations. Keep future metadata support. Offer a straightforward playable-only option or separate concepts from recordings, retaining the custom banner player and both downloads. Do not relabel a concept as available.

**Decision needed:** Approval for filtering improvements; keeping concepts visible by default is a design choice, not a canon question.

### R20. Reduce navigation work in long readers

**Evidence:** A Chapter reader places the full horizontal story timeline before the actual chapter. Long documents and profiles require substantial scrolling; the desktop layout also has an independently scrolling content pane and a Page notes column. These structures work, but they compete with sustained reading.

**Proposed action:** Offer a compact or collapsible timeline in reader views, retain the full timeline in catalogs, add useful section navigation, and preserve back navigation and scroll position. Improve the use of the right column without adding a permanent left sidebar. Do not remove the timeline or breadcrumbs.

**Decision needed:** Approval for reader-focused design work. A default reading mode should be chosen together with R07.

### R21. Finish accessibility testing beyond visual correctness

**Evidence:** Existing tests cover menu focus and several interactions, but do not establish complete screen-reader behavior, contrast, every tooltip dismissal route, map rearrangement without a pointer, or physical iPhone gesture behavior. The local WebKit run is not native Safari certification.

**Proposed action:** Test F/M/S notes with keyboard and assistive technology, verify text contrast and touch targets, test map and portrait interaction under zoom and reduced motion, and confirm that drag gestures do not trap vertical scrolling. Add only the controls required to make the existing interactions usable.

**Decision needed:** Approval for the accessibility pass. A physical Safari check requires access to the target environment; until then, report that limit explicitly.

## Writer workflow and cohesion

### R22. Make the ledgers reflect evidence and actual unresolved decisions

**Evidence:** The contradictions ledger records no unresolved contradiction despite the source drift in R01 and the boundary problem requiring review in R02. The capability ledger already holds several concerns that have not changed the graph. Confidence percentages help scan the questions but do not explain what evidence would close them.

**Proposed action:** Register verified conflicts after approval, link them to their owning sources and required follow-up surfaces, and group questions by the decision that would answer them. Retain the required confidence and resolution bars. Separate ordinary missing information, deliberate mysteries, and genuinely incompatible statements. Do not resolve a deliberate contradiction in Lester's existence as though it were a continuity error.

**Decision needed:** Approval for ledger maintenance. Answers remain the author's unless already established.

### R23. Reduce duplicated explanations and conflicting maintenance guidance

**Evidence:** Suppression doctrine and basic ontology are explained in several World and Docs records. They serve different readers, but repeated full explanations increase drift risk. The maintenance charter still describes entity links as ordinary prose until hover, while the recorded design preference is that they remain ordinary-looking in every state, with usable keyboard focus. Large shared style sections and repeated navigation markup also make local adjustments hard to reason about.

**Proposed action:** Keep one owning definition and concise contextual explanations elsewhere. Reconcile guidance wording, document the owner of each recurring component, and refactor only where tests demonstrate equivalent behavior. Do not force the individually designed World pages into one template or undertake a framework migration.

**Decision needed:** Approval for focused maintenance and source consolidation, not a site rebuild.

### R24. Extend regression checks to the failures this audit found

**Evidence:** Existing checks passed while the 1024-pixel text overflows and Lester's foundation drift remained. A single explicitly registered v1 document also exposed a mismatch between search URL generation and the expected version URL during today's integration; the new reference follows the existing implicit-v1 convention instead.

**Proposed action:** Add tests for internal text overflow at intermediate widths, explicit single-version documents, visible artwork era labels, and selected cross-record canon invariants. Retain current checks for snapshots, search history, links, gradients, masonry, and mobile navigation. Keep prose judgment and unresolved canon out of false pass/fail assertions.

**Decision needed:** Implementation approval only for tests and demonstrated defects; do not turn a hypothesis into a test that forces it to become canon.
