# Comprehensive Content Audit: 30 September 2026

## Assessment

MAGIARCHY has convincing individual encounters, recognizable central characters, and an increasingly specific contemporary setting. Its largest weakness is the distance between the developed scenes and the causal structure connecting them. The river operation, office-building rescue, recruitment interview, and several relationship exchanges are substantially more developed than the assassination, fugitive campaign, Academy crisis, and political settlement that must hold the main story together.

This audit records **25 findings**. They include correctable wording and record conflicts, decisions that require the author, and editorial recommendations. They are not 25 confirmed canon contradictions. Nothing in this document authorizes a rewrite, selects an unresolved outcome, or changes an existing default version.

**Recommended first work:** settle the assassination and exoneration dependencies (09–10), remove the unsupported certainty around Myka's placement (02), establish the knowledge boundaries for unplaced encounters (12), and reconcile the river's flame preparation with the protective-glove rule (01). Then develop the Academy/Cardiff strand and the missing dream material (11, 13).

## Scope and method

The review covers the working archive as it stood on 30 September 2026:

| Gallery and Music | Public descriptions, labels, scene associations, and canon boundaries, including all 9 panel collections. This is a content-metadata review, not a fresh anatomical inspection of every image or a listening review of every recording. |
| Home and discovery | The factual snapshots, character and genre summaries, editorial task lists, and current/default versus historical distinctions. |

The prose warning tool also examined 56 current sources and 4,442 passages. Its 79 warnings were used as prompts for review, not as verdicts. Repetition in a maintenance document or an approved comic exchange is not automatically a defect.

The audit excludes CSS, layout, code architecture, performance, and general interaction design. Source implementation was inspected only where it determines a content claim, such as what a character-presence count actually counts. Private workshop and unpublished generation studies were not treated as public story evidence.

### Priorities and classifications

- **P1:** resolve before drafting the dependent major strand or treating the affected record as settled.
- **P2:** correct or decide during the next relevant content revision.
- **P3:** lower-risk editorial housekeeping.
- **Record conflict:** current records make incompatible or differently scoped claims.
- **Continuity risk:** necessary information is absent; contradiction has not been proved.
- **Author decision:** the archive deliberately lacks an answer.
- **Editorial recommendation:** a judgment about execution, not a new canon fact.

Existing open questions retain their existing confidence. An audit recommendation is not evidence that an answer has become more certain. A finding is closed only when its named sources agree or the author explicitly declines the recommendation.

## Record corrections and continuity risks

### 01. River blue flame does not establish glove preparation

**Evidence:** [The Empty Boats Beneath the Bridge v3](../story.html?chapter=the-empty-boats-beneath-the-bridge&version=v3) changes Lynleit into a blue suede jacket, puts her underwater, and says, “Blue flame broke from both hands.” It never establishes that she has put on the protective gloves. The [glove record](../items.html?item=lynleits-protective-gloves), [production guidance](../docs.html?doc=character-image-production), and [capability reference](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance) now require them during outbursts to protect her skin.

**Why it matters:** the later-confirmed rule applies beyond the office rescue. A reader should not have to invent equipment preparation to reconcile two major demonstrations of the same ability. The river text does not explicitly say that her hands are bare, so this is a preparation gap rather than proof of an impossible unprotected discharge.

**Resolution:** decide whether she was already wearing the gloves for this operation or knowingly uses the flame without them in an emergency. The former needs a brief, natural preparation detail; the latter needs the author to settle the resulting injury. Do not invent protection from the water, transfer the coat's properties to the suede jacket, or impose the office rescue's exact symptoms on every use.

**Close when:** the Chapter, river Moment, incident account, and relevant image briefs describe one compatible sequence. Existing approved artwork need not be repainted merely to close the prose gap.

### 02. Myka's unconfirmed recruitment period occupies a fixed Arc 2 phase

**Evidence:** [Myka Enters MSF](../moments.html?moment=myka-enters-msf) assigns `kyriens-burden`, the thread “MSF after Lynleit's disappearance,” and Kyrien's director timeline anchor. Its own continuity text says that recruitment during his directorate remains uncertain. The confirmed facts establish survival, graduation at the top of her class, and subsequent MSF recruitment, not that specific period or recruiter.

**Why it matters:** the structured placement tells a stronger story than the qualification. It also gives Kyrien an apparent participation in an event whose recruitment agent is unknown. This is precisely the kind of approximate strand that should not occupy a numbered track merely to fill one.

**Resolution:** either obtain confirmation of Arc 2/directorate placement or move the event to an approximate/unplaced strand while retaining its known order after the Academy and graduation. Review the Kyrien participation tag separately from his possible institutional connection.

**Close when:** Story, Moments, both profiles, and Home's coverage no longer imply an unapproved date or personal recruitment.

### 03. Water-walking is still described as secret from everyone

**Evidence:** [Lynleit's profile](../character.html?character=lynleit), “Night practice,” says that she “keeps the talent secret from everyone.” The current river Chapter and profile establish Kyrien as a witness. Home takes this same field as its water-walking fact.

**Why it matters:** an absolute present-tense claim conflicts with the later disclosure and propagates into a standalone snapshot where its period is not apparent.

**Resolution:** scope the statement to her solitary practice before the river, or say that she conceals it from the Church and wider circle while acknowledging the known witness. Do not add other people to the secret.

**Close when:** a reader encountering the profile or Home fact alone can distinguish concealment from universal ignorance.

### 04. Sleepers puts Kyrien's hands in a pocketless jacket

**Evidence:** [Kyrien's profile](../character.html?character=kyrien) describes his approved beige jacket as having “no pockets.” [Sleepers above the River v2](../moments.html?moment=sleepers-above-the-river&version=v2) opens with his hands “in his jacket pockets.” The [illustrated sequence](../gallery.html?panels=sleepers-above-the-river) uses his recognizable light-jacket appearance.

**Why it matters:** this is a small but concrete contradiction between a deliberate design constraint and physical staging. It can be resolved without changing the scene's tone.

**Resolution:** confirm the jacket variant. If it is the approved pocketless design, change the hand position, not the garment. If another jacket is intended, identify that variation rather than silently adding pockets to the standard design.

**Close when:** prose and the named outfit agree; do not regenerate the panel set to repair a sentence.

### 05. Lester's Magecraft score contradicts the category of his ability

**Evidence:** [Lester](../character.html?character=lester) is a demiholumn whose anomalous nature is not trained magecraft. The [Capability Balance ledger](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance#balance-contradictions) already records that his Magecraft score of 91 represents the wrong kind of ability.

**Why it matters:** a provisional number can still teach the wrong ontology. Lowering the number does not solve the category mistake. Similar limits affect Mikhail's counter-Magus competence and Kyrien's later abilities presented without a life-period distinction.

**Resolution:** decide how anomalous nature and Church countermeasures should be represented, or leave an inapplicable axis unscored. Do not grant Lester learned magecraft to justify the graph. Treat Fionn's unexplained vulnerabilities as missing characterization, not something a revised number can supply.

**Close when:** comparison labels distinguish kinds and periods of competence without changing their canon.

### 06. Sherie's profile appearance lags behind her registered design

**Evidence:** [Sherie's profile](../character.html?character=sherie) still summarizes “Tailored fieldwear with compact support gear and visible communications equipment,” with a navy/slate/emergency-blue palette. Her [Gallery artwork](../gallery.html?character=sherie) and the [card-game prose](../moments.html?moment=unresolved-tension) emphasize a different recognizable wardrobe, including the black tailored ensemble and ruffled cuffs.

**Why it matters:** an old generic operational description can misdirect future artwork even while the portrait shows the approved design. A field outfit and an off-duty outfit can coexist; the discrepancy does not prove that one must be deleted.

**Resolution:** compare the profile description with the selected reference and approved variants. Describe the main design accurately and label any separately approved operational outfit. Review Felix's similarly generic fieldwear description during the same bounded pass, without redesigning either character.

**Close when:** the profile is a reliable description of an identified approved outfit rather than an unsourced replacement for it.

### 07. Character presence includes people linked to a scene but absent from it

**Evidence:** Home's [character chart](../index.html) calls its measure “Scene presence,” while its explanation correctly says it counts Moment character lists. [The Nameless Street](../moments.html?moment=the-nameless-street) includes Fionn and Helena because of later consequences, although neither acts in that Chapter. [Myka's recruitment](../moments.html?moment=myka-enters-msf) includes Kyrien while his personal recruitment role remains a question.

**Why it matters:** the arithmetic is not the problem. The label can make associated characters look as though they have received actual scene time. A writer trying to find underdeveloped characters may draw the wrong conclusion.

**Resolution:** distinguish appearance from mention/consequence/institutional association, or call the current measure linked-Moment coverage. Preserve useful character links without treating them all as on-page presence. Do not reinterpret the bars as minutes or literary complexity.

**Close when:** inspecting a count reveals what kind of involvement it represents. The existing method note should remain.

### 08. The contradiction overview omits unresolved capability conflicts

**Evidence:** [Contradictions to Resolve](../docs.html?doc=contradictions-to-resolve) reports no unresolved contradictions, while [Capability Balance](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance#balance-contradictions) retains unresolved rows, including Lester's misclassified ability. Home's contradiction snapshot reads the former ledger only.

**Why it matters:** the ledgers have different scopes, but the combined impression can be that the archive has no outstanding conflicts. Some balance rows are development questions rather than strict contradictions, making an automatic merge equally misleading.

**Resolution:** distinguish unresolved canon contradictions, representation conflicts, and capability-development questions. Give the overview a concise route to the separate balance issues. If accepted, add only genuine new contradictions from this report to the living ledger, not every recommendation.

**Close when:** “none recorded” clearly states its scope and does not hide acknowledged problems elsewhere.

## Decisions and missing story connections

### 09. Fionn's assassination lacks its causal chain

**Evidence:** [The Unresolved Assassination](../moments.html?moment=fionn-is-eliminated) confirms converging incompatible agendas, Helena's knowing assistance with one part, and an uncertain fall. It does not yet establish the participants' concrete objectives, actions, access, or mistaken expectations. [Questions to Be Answered](../docs.html?doc=questions-to-be-answered) already preserves these choices.

**Why it matters:** the central mystery cannot be fairly planted or eventually explained from the existence of several interested factions alone. Fionn's high competence makes the missing mechanism especially important.

**Resolution:** privately settle who intends what, what each actually does, what each knows, and which unintended interactions produce the death. Separately determine the physical cause and what investigators can recover. Preserve accidental cooperation and partial knowledge; do not simplify this into a single mastermind.

**Close when:** the author can trace the outcome from specific actions and identify earlier observable clues without exposing the solution prematurely to the reader. This remains a major carry-forward from the September content audit.

### 10. Reclaiming MSF and disproving murder remain different unfinished tasks

**Evidence:** [The First Lines Back into MSF](../moments.html?moment=the-first-lines-back-into-msf), [MSF](../msf.html), and [Lynleit's profile](../character.html?character=lynleit) establish loyalist contact, blue-flame authentication, Helena's control, and a continuing hunt. The campaign's decisive evidence, institutional reversals, and final settlement remain thin.

**Why it matters:** proving that a message really comes from Lynleit does not prove she did not kill Fionn. Recovering practical control of a company does not itself cancel an accusation or establish magical succession.

**Resolution:** develop separate but connected paths for personal authentication, officer loyalty, material exoneration, state response, and Magiarchal recognition. Decide what Helena loses, retains, or conceals at each stage. Preserve ordinary law and the private company's particular arrangements rather than letting magecraft answer every jurisdictional question.

**Close when:** each reversal is caused by evidence, leverage, or a specific decision, with a clear remaining cost. No new legal procedure is proposed here.

### 11. Cardiff and the Academy have outcomes but insufficient operational substance

**Evidence:** [Father Mikhail's Foreign Venture](../moments.html?moment=father-mikhails-foreign-venture) and [Terrors inside the Academy](../moments.html?moment=terrors-inside-the-academy) establish a simultaneous strain: Lynleit accompanies Mikhail abroad, while Kyrien helps Myka in her place. The mission, adversaries, access conditions, and reasons the Academy cannot solve its own problem are unresolved.

**Why it matters:** these strands need to show why their particular participants are necessary. Otherwise Cardiff becomes a removal device for Lynleit and Myka becomes a person waiting for rescue.

**Resolution:** settle the task and failure pressure in Cardiff; the Academy's concrete danger; how a non-Magus can enter and contribute; what Myka notices or chooses that Kyrien cannot; and what failure costs each group. Place these pressures coherently before Fionn's death without inventing exact dates simply to make the timeline neat.

**Close when:** both operations have a legible objective, access route, character-specific contribution, and consequence. Myka's later strength must arise through more than the fact of graduating first.

### 12. Several encounters need knowledge boundaries before dates

**Evidence:** [Kyrien's origin reference](../docs.html?doc=kyrien-origin-and-recruitment) protects the river as his discovery of magic. [Sleepers](../moments.html?moment=sleepers-above-the-river) is unplaced despite his observing unsupported human figures. [The Shared Night](../moments.html?moment=the-shared-night) requires Kyrien and Sherie to know about magic; Sherie's prior discovery is not written. Drake's later understanding of the river also needs a route from witnesses to adviser.

**Why it matters:** an unplaced event can still have prerequisites. Leaving the date open must not make every relative order equally possible.

**Resolution:** record the minimum knowledge constraints. Decide how Kyrien initially understands the Sleepers and whether the encounter must follow the river revelation; do not casually grant earlier magical knowledge. Choose how Sherie learns enough to attend Natalia's briefing and what Drake is told about the failed interception. Keep Hiyu and Yulia outside that briefing and the early magic-aware circle.

**Close when:** each scene has an explicit knowledge-before/knowledge-after boundary even if its calendar placement stays approximate.

### 13. The Shared Night still needs its missing experience and wider consequence

**Evidence:** [The Shared Night v2](../story.html?chapter=the-shared-night&version=v2) contains the acknowledged writer gap for Lynleit and Kyrien. The [research record](../docs.html?doc=oneiric-confluence) leaves the outbreak's selection, duration, and ending open. The opening's reports across government departments reach Natalia, but the collection and access that make her briefing possible are not yet concretely established.

**Why it matters:** the missing dream is intended to change two people, not merely fill a slot beside Sherie and Felix. The final private success also does not explain what becomes of the wider public incident.

**Resolution:** select the nonsexual, painful or emotionally exposing event and what each person actually learns. Childhood trauma is an option, not an instruction to invent it. Establish a credible route for the reports and decide what practical consequence follows the briefing and subsequent dream. Keep recognition a limited opening for choice, not a reliable cure.

**Close when:** the gap is replaced by approved content, its relationship consequence is specific, and the wider incident has either an outcome or an intentional continuing pressure. Sherie and Felix's no-kissing boundary remains intact.

### 14. The early rescue's entry and aftermath need connective scenes

**Evidence:** [There Is No Eleventh Floor](../story.html?chapter=there-is-no-eleventh-floor) makes Lynleit's arrival effective from the witness's limited viewpoint. [Fionn's office reprimand](../moments.html?moment=fionns-office-after-the-rescue) is still an outline. Natalia's information route and the proposed immediate [cat chase](../gallery.html?panels=cat-incident) connection remain questions.

**Why it matters:** the witness need not learn how she found him, but the author needs a compatible answer before using the rescue as evidence of her judgment or readiness. Fionn's objection must concern her risk, not suddenly condemn her for helping someone.

**Resolution:** determine what brings Lynleit inside and how Natalia learns enough to notify Fionn. Draft the reprimand around her defence that she followed Natalia's instructions and his revelation that Natalia told him. Decide, rather than assume, whether Ash distracts her immediately afterward. If it does, let the subsequent roof risk complicate the lesson without narrating a moral over the cat chase.

**Close when:** the aftermath works as a character scene and the chronology does not introduce later Magi or give outsiders premature knowledge. The lost pistol's later custody or replacement can be settled alongside equipment continuity without requiring a retrieval adventure.

### 15. The park pause needs practical context and the flask needs earlier familiarity

**Evidence:** [The Bench under the Lamp v2](../story.html?chapter=the-bench-under-the-lamp&version=v2) follows work and takes place in a park whose danger persists. The [question ledger](../docs.html?doc=questions-to-be-answered) explicitly asks what work and precautions permit the pause. [Kyrien's flask](../items.html?item=kyriens-whiskey-flask) is recurring equipment, but its strongest developed scene use is still this same payoff.

**Why it matters:** unexplained relaxation in an active danger area can weaken the competence established elsewhere. A biography calling an object familiar is not the same as the reader recognizing it before the joke.

**Resolution:** choose the immediate work context and a modest, concrete reason they can pause there. Establish the flask through an earlier appropriate moment of exceptional strain, without making alcohol routine, therapeutic, or the source of Kyrien's competence. Preserve the bench departure before Lynleit's request and do not explain the joke afterward.

**Close when:** the pause feels like these people making a situated choice, and the flask is recognizable without an origin lecture. The undrafted intimate passage remains a separate writer gap.

### 16. The family reveal requires separate reader and character milestones

**Evidence:** [Reader Knowledge](../docs.html?doc=reader-knowledge), [The End of the First Arc](../moments.html?moment=the-end-of-the-first-arc), and the [Intimacy Reference](../docs.html?doc=character-intimacy-and-sexuality) preserve pregnancy near the Arc transition, inference at the Arc 2 opening, a concealed son, later reconnection, and a subsequent daughter. They do not settle who assists concealment, when Kyrien learns, or which events restore intimacy.

**Why it matters:** reader inference, a character's suspicion, explicit discovery, and public disclosure are four different events. Compressing them would remove much of the intended emotional and political pressure.

**Resolution:** specify the evidence available at each threshold, the people who know, and the practical circumstances of concealment. Choose the Arc 2 decisions that rebuild the relationship before treating the second child as its resolution. Do not assume inherited powers or move the daughter's revelation into Arc 2.

**Close when:** the planned sequence can be followed without a direct pregnancy announcement at the protected early reveal, while Kyrien's later understanding has its own cause and consequence.

### 17. Anomalous capabilities need usable limits without exhaustive explanations

**Evidence:** [Capability Balance](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance), [Lester](../character.html?character=lester), [Items & Artefacts](../items.html), and the [Holumn incident archive](../docs.html?doc=holumn-incidents-and-testimonies) leave important practical limits open: Lester's interventions, the coat's cooperation, blue-flame endurance, and several manifestations' escape conditions.

**Why it matters:** ignorance of origin can sustain dread. Ignorance of every relevant limitation can instead allow any rescue or defeat to happen when convenient.

**Resolution:** before a new encounter, choose only the operative facts it needs: an observable trace, an available action, a local limit, and a consequence of misjudgment. Lester's impossible origin should remain impossible; it need not become an experiment or transformation. The coat must not become an obedient universal shield. Bone Archive needs a particular encounter and cost before becoming a solution to someone else's history.

**Close when:** a reader can follow why an action is attempted and what it risks without receiving a universal supernatural manual. The river surface boundary, office escape, and dream recognition must retain their local evidential limits.

### 18. Several secondary strands lack consequences owned by their characters

**Evidence:** [Heyk](../character.html?character=heyk) survives a massacre and later serves Drake and Sherie; [Hiyu](../character.html?character=hiyu) and [Yulia](../character.html?character=yulia) enter the quarantine; [Inspector Leo](../character.html?character=inspector-leo) faces restricted knowledge; [Tien](../character.html?character=tien) serves Helena as a concealed assassin. Their circumstances are distinct, but decisive later choices and consequences are less developed than their roles.

**Why it matters:** a specialist can become an instrument of another person's plot if the archive only describes usefulness. The students' surviving the park or learning magic must not be assumed merely because they have profiles and artwork.

**Resolution:** give Heyk something specific he brings out or cannot reliably report; choose what the students' entry changes and what remains unknown to them; give Leo a consequential investigative decision under his actual clearance; and identify a limit or choice for Tien beyond being highly effective. These are separate decisions, not a demand for equal page counts or a shared trauma pattern.

**Close when:** each developed strand has at least one consequence arising from that person's method, mistake, loyalty, or refusal. Resolve the student's encounter before casually reusing them as magic-aware investigators.

## Prose, emphasis, and reader experience

### 19. Doom states reciprocal identity change more clearly than it dramatizes it

**Evidence:** [Doom Has an Address v7](../story.html?chapter=doom-has-an-address&version=v7) gives Natalia's hypothesis, Mikhail's confrontation, the incomplete attempt left off-page, and subsequent recovery. Its explanation identifies change in both egos; Lynleit's embarrassment and revised relation to Kyrien are more available to the reader than his corresponding inward alteration.

**Why it matters:** a metaphysical outcome can feel asserted even when its mechanism is well explained. This was already a concern in the [earlier content audit](../docs.html?doc=content-audit-2026-09-19), and the successful dialogue should not be sacrificed to address it.

**Resolution:** consider a specific changed choice or perception from Kyrien around the existing before-and-after structure. The author must select that change. No explicit depiction of the omitted encounter is required, and it must not become first intercourse or a general cure for the curse category.

**Close when:** the reader can recognize a change in both people without a second explanatory lecture or an invented event inserted as fact.

### 20. The Shared Night's extended briefing can outlast its dramatic purpose

**Evidence:** [The Shared Night v2](../story.html?chapter=the-shared-night&version=v2) sustains a long group exchange around the intimate dream reports before returning to the private test. The early accounts establish violence, grief, and ordinary experience, but those other pressures become less immediate during the extended banter.

**Why it matters:** comedy and embarrassment are strengths here. The risk is that the scene's ongoing danger becomes a topic of discussion rather than something that affects what the group must do next. Many similar reaction beats can also reduce the contrast between speakers.

**Resolution:** mark what changes at each major turn: new evidence, a hypothesis rejected, an exposure between characters, or a practical choice. Preserve approved jokes, interruptions, and productive repetitions. Where two stretches do the same work, consider redistributing one rather than mechanically shortening every exchange. Let Natalia's useful worked reasoning remain distinct from the others' embarrassment.

**Close when:** the author can identify why the briefing continues at each stage and what the participants leave intending to do. This is an editorial judgment, not a finding that the chapter has too much humor or must obey a fixed genre ratio.

### 21. Explanatory verdicts sometimes repeat what the exchange already conveys

**Evidence:** The Shared Night includes the generalizing beat, “six intelligent adults simultaneously understood that somebody had just stepped onto a landmine.” The office rescue sometimes retrospectively explains Lynleit's method after action has made it legible. Current profiles and scene summaries also accumulate qualifications around what an event does not establish.

**Why it matters:** a narrator's verdict can replace the particular reaction the reader came to see. Repeated qualifications can make a lively character or unsettling incident read like an editorial defence.

**Resolution:** during a local voice pass, ask whether the line supplies new perception, a distinctive joke, or necessary uncertainty. Keep it when it does. Otherwise trust the action and remove the duplicate explanation. Preserve the office witness's first-person retrospective voice; do not force it into the usual third-person mode. Treat the prose tool's warnings as review prompts only.

**Close when:** any revision reads better beside the author-approved source and has not erased banter, asymmetry, callbacks, or a witness's characteristic phrasing.

### 22. Written-scene coverage underrepresents the central political story

**Evidence:** The [Story catalog](../story.html) has substantial supernatural encounters and relationship dialogue, while the assassination, takeover, loyalist campaign, and major institutional confrontations are principally carried by [Moment outlines](../moments.html). Home's genre chart explicitly represents editorial emphasis, not measured manuscript proportions.

**Why it matters:** the desired mix of magic, politics, horror, espionage, and intimacy exists in direction documents more evenly than in finished scene experience. This is a development imbalance, not evidence that the existing intimate or comic material should be cut.

**Resolution:** prioritize a political scene that makes a settled decision costly: the rejected warning, an institutional refusal, a loyalist contact, or a specific reversal during the fugitive campaign. Choose it after resolving the relevant causal questions. Use professional work, ordinary inconvenience, and personal loyalties to give politics the same immediacy as the river operation.

**Close when:** the main political conflict has consequential scenes, not just longer summaries. Do not turn Home's theme weights into writing quotas.

### 23. Seasonal and fog direction needs selective scene-level implementation

**Evidence:** [Thematic Direction](../docs.html?doc=thematic-direction) makes the late-summer approach to warm golden autumn important. [Dread and Action Direction](../docs.html?doc=dread-and-action-direction) establishes recurring regional fog and unusual incident mist. The opening has a September anchor, and the river and park already use impaired visibility, but most unplaced episodes have no selected seasonal context.

**Why it matters:** the atmosphere can otherwise remain strongest in instructions and artwork rather than in the developing narrative. The opposite mistake would be to recolor every encounter into one autumn or make every fog bank a supernatural signal.

**Resolution:** once a scene's relative period is known, choose a few material details appropriate to its viewpoint: light on Lynleit's blue clothing, leaves under wet shoes, an obscured bank, or the usable sound of a route. Select actual fog-prone locations when geography becomes relevant. Do not invent meteorological mechanisms, force golden light into a night rescue, or compress an Arc that may span years.

**Close when:** season and visibility affect a character's perception or action in selected scenes, while contemporary life, scene-specific weather, and chronological uncertainty remain intact.

## Catalog boundaries and unfinished material

### 24. The invented archive Chapter needs an explicit retention decision

**Evidence:** [The Name Left Behind](../story.html?chapter=the-name-left-behind) is correctly labelled a placeholder. It nevertheless supplies a missing-name archive, Myka's preserved copy, Fionn beside a future date, and self-erasing evidence, with a specific Story phase and neutral event table.

**Why it matters:** these are substantial plot propositions, not harmless temporary prose. Repeated exposure through the catalog can make them feel established even without a formal canon claim. The current label prevents calling this a hidden contradiction, but it does not answer whether the idea should remain in the plan.

**Resolution:** have the author retain, replace, or retire this invented Chapter before developing its apparent clues. Until then, do not use its future date or erasing archive to explain Fionn's death, Myka's predicament, or another Holumn. Preserve the historical file if it is retired.

**Close when:** the Chapter has an explicit editorial disposition and its invented events are not silently promoted into shared canon. This is a carry-forward development decision, not a request to delete history.

### 25. Gallery and Music associations must not silently settle story facts

**Evidence:** [After the River](../gallery.html?panels=after-the-river) introduces an overhead shape not identified by the current Chapter; the [question ledger](../docs.html?doc=questions-to-be-answered) correctly leaves its identity and integration open. [University Days](../gallery.html?panels=hiyu-yulia-university-days) does not establish a specific police case or official role. [Darkness](../gallery.html?panels=darkness) has a title and pairing but no images or selected event.
