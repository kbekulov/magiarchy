# Character Aphorisms

Distinctive sayings and turns of phrase from MAGIARCHY's characters. The lines below are collected for future dialogue; no scene or listener has been assigned.

## Sherie

### Catastrophe and catharsis

> "When men and women play games, the prize is somewhere between catastrophe and catharsis. Nature does enjoy a gamble."

### Schrödinger's buttocks

> "Schrödinger's buttocks: she may want to get to know you better, or kick your head in. With Lynleit, you won't know until she moves."

## Writer's use

These two lines adapt the author's supplied wording for [Sherie](../character.html?character=sherie). Her remark about [Lynleit](../character.html?character=lynleit) is teasing, not evidence of Lynleit's feelings toward a particular person. Keep both sayings separate from the existing [card-game exchange](../moments.html?moment=unresolved-tension) until a scene calls for them.
