# Site, Story and Artwork Audit: 3 October 2026

## Assessment and first actions

The strongest material is still the close encounter: people trying to understand a danger while dealing with another person, an awkward obligation, or an object that will not cooperate. The River Choir trilogy now gives both Kyrien and Lynleit sustained first-person experience. The office-building rescue, recruitment interview and church exchanges also have distinctive practical detail. The greatest remaining story risk is connective causality: the developed encounters are much more definite than the assassination, institutional response and political settlement joining them.

This is a new audit of the current site, not a replacement for its earlier audit history. Findings distinguish **record conflicts**, **author decisions**, **editorial recommendations** and **artwork repairs**. Recommendations do not establish canon. Historical revisions, an archive's explanation and a character's own knowledge are different things.

Recommended order:

1. Correct the two stale river knowledge references in findings 01 and 02, and resolve the Myka placement conflict in 03.
2. Make the assassination-to-exoneration chain concrete before expanding its dependent scenes (09–11).
3. Resolve the Academy/Cardiff operational dependencies and the missing shared-dream material (12–14).
4. Reconcile capability displays and outfit/weapon continuity (04–06, 18–20).
5. Use the artwork revision register below to review v2s beside their preserved originals. Items awaiting an author decision must not be reported as repaired.

## Scope and limits

The baseline is the working archive at commit `a795ede`, with the current production instructions and author-supplied design changes checked separately. The review covers all 18 character profiles, all 12 current/default Chapters, all 33 current Moments, the World overview and its eight linked records, 10 Holumn incidents, 3 item records, 6 weapon records, the current writer guidance and living ledgers, Gallery metadata, Music descriptions, Home summaries and discovery metadata. Historical versions were consulted for continuity and checked as historical destinations; they were not all line-edited or treated as competing current canon.

Artwork review covered **243 active original images** at the baseline: 92 general/seasonal/Fan Service artworks, 46 chibis, 62 panel images, 30 production-resource images, and 13 other registered images. Every original received a visual contact-sheet inspection; potential mismatches and correction targets were opened at larger scale. This is a face/hair and cross-media design review, not a claim of pixel-level anatomical certification for every background figure. Private workshop studies, generation attempts and backlog inputs are not public artwork and were not intaken.

The prose checklist was run separately for the four current first-person Chapters. Editorial judgments appear below; a successful command does not itself pass the prose. Music was reviewed for descriptions, assignments, availability and navigation, not by listening to every complete recording. Automated source, link, identity, preservation and browser checks complement rather than replace the reading and visual review.

## Correctable record and presentation issues

### 01. Water walking is not secret from everyone

**Record conflict · high priority.** Lynleit's profile ability card, “Night practice,” says she keeps the talent secret from everyone. In [The Wrong Bank](../story.html?chapter=the-wrong-bank), Kyrien watches her emerge and move on the water; the profile's wider continuity also records his concealed knowledge. The card can feed Home fact snapshots, so the isolated sentence travels beyond the profile.

**Resolve:** distinguish Lynleit's intention and belief from the archive's knowledge. She practises privately and does not know how much Kyrien saw. Do not remove his observation, announce a confession to him, or weaken the Church danger to resolve the wording. Owner: [Lynleit](../character.html?character=lynleit), ability card in `character.js`.

### 02. The reveal guide still points to the pre-split river Chapter

**Stale guidance · high priority.** The fog discussion in [Reader Knowledge](../docs.html?doc=reader-knowledge) still names river Chapter v3, while the current sequence is Part I v4, Part II and Part III. This can send a future writer back to an omniscient account when deciding what either first-person narrator can know.

**Resolve:** map each relevant sighting to the current part: Kyrien's first encounter and escape; Lynleit's underwater experience; Kyrien's concealed observation and reunion. Retain v3 as history rather than silently calling it current.

### 03. Myka's displayed phase is more definite than her placement

**Placement conflict · high priority.** [Myka joins MSF](../moments.html?moment=myka-enters-msf) is attached to the phase `kyriens-burden`, but its placement remains provisional and its relation to Lynleit's disappearance is still a question. A numbered Arc 2 position implies an answer the continuity record has not settled.

**Resolve:** choose the event's placement, or move it to an approximate strand while retaining any genuinely known Arc information. Do not move the Academy crisis or invent a date just to make the join line fit. Check the Moment, character timeline, Story phase and current search summary together.

### 04. Lester's Magecraft score contradicts his classification

**Record conflict · high priority, already recognized but unresolved.** [Lester](../character.html?character=lester) has a Magecraft graph value of 91 while his demiholumn nature is explicitly inherent rather than magecraft. [Character Capability Balance](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance) already records this conflict. A “provisional” disclaimer does not make the axis describe him correctly.

**Resolve:** decide whether the graph needs a distinct anomalous-nature axis, a non-applicable value, or a different comparison. Do not replace 91 with another invented rating. Keep his unknown origin and intrinsic chronological contradiction separate from the practical limits of what he can do.

### 05. Some appearance summaries lag behind approved artwork

**Incomplete records · medium priority.** Lester's physical fields still leave hair, eyes, build and identifying details unspecified despite the public design decision and approved sketch. Sherie's short visual summary still foregrounds compact support gear and visible communications equipment, unlike the dominant approved tailored outfit. Leo and Mikhail also have more visible silhouette information than their brief physical records convey.

**Resolve:** describe only what the approved art actually establishes: clothing, hair construction, visible build and recurring equipment. Do not infer an exact height or eye colour from monochrome sketches. Keep alternate clothing separate from the identity package and do not use a Fan Service pose to establish habitual field clothing. Owners: [Lester](../character.html?character=lester), [Sherie](../character.html?character=sherie), [Leo](../character.html?character=inspector-leo), [Mikhail](../character.html?character=father-mikhail).

### 06. Capability values lack a common time and scale

**Presentation limitation · medium priority.** The profiles correctly call their assessment provisional, but values still combine opening competence, eventual development and incomparable abilities. Kyrien's later intelligence work can read as an opening baseline. Mikhail's Church countermeasures disappear into broad ordinary axes. Fionn's high values say little about exploitable dependencies.

**Resolve:** agree what the comparison measures and when, before adjusting scores. Prefer scene evidence and limits over artificial numerical differentiation. The existing [balance ledger](../docs.html?doc=character-capability-balance) is the owning decision record; this audit does not create a second score system.

### 07. Home's empty contradiction snapshot has a narrow source

**Discovery limitation · medium priority.** Home draws unresolved contradictions only from the main Contradictions ledger. That ledger is empty at the baseline, while the capability ledger contains five unresolved rows and the present audit identifies additional record conflicts. The empty message is literally scoped to listed contradictions, but the broader dashboard can feel like a clean bill of health.

**Resolve:** name the source “Main contradiction ledger,” or provide a separate link/count for outstanding audit findings. Do not count every open question as a contradiction or silently combine advisory proposals with confirmed incompatibilities.

### 08. Character bars count associations, not every actual appearance

**Measurement limitation · medium priority.** The Home methodology correctly disclaims minutes, dialogue shares and literary-complexity scores. Nevertheless, the label “Scene presence” is stronger than its underlying operation: each character listed on a current Moment receives a count. Mentioned commissioners or background institutional figures can count without appearing in the prose; incomplete drafts count too.

**Resolve:** either call this “Moment associations” consistently, or distinguish on-scene appearance, off-scene involvement and reference-only membership in the source records. Keep the visible evidence links. The genre chart is already clearly labelled editorial emphasis and explicitly not a measured story ratio; that is a strength, not an error to fix.

## Decisions needed for the main story

### 09. Specify the causal chain around Fionn's death

**Author decision · high priority.** [Fionn is eliminated](../moments.html?moment=fionn-is-eliminated), the profiles and institutional records establish several agendas, concealed roles and consequences. They do not yet provide the complete chain by which one decision enables the next: who initiates which act, what Tien does, what Helena knows, and which evidence the survivors can obtain.

**Next action:** make a private event/evidence/knowledge table for the assassination and immediate takeover. Give each link an actor, a means, a limit and a discoverable trace. Preserve the multi-actor conspiracy; do not make Drake, Helena or Fionn secretly responsible for every convenient outcome.

### 10. Reconnecting with MSF does not itself clear the fugitives

**Missing causal bridge · high priority.** Trusted officers recognizing Lynleit and Kyrien, restored communications, reversing public accusations, and regaining institutional control are different achievements. The current broad outline advances between them without specifying the evidence and authority that each step requires.

**Next action:** choose what the group can prove, to whom, and why that recipient can act. The state can buy MSF's services without surrendering all criminal jurisdiction. Keep the limits negotiated in [After the Failed Attempt](../story.html?chapter=after-the-failed-attempt), rather than granting magical or corporate immunity retroactively.

### 11. Decide which external restraints on MSF actually work

**World-development decision · high priority.** [MSF](../msf.html) and [Narvea](../duchy.html) establish private succession, government contracts, concentrated officers in Turon and substantial latent coercive power. Officer decency is explicitly a major safeguard. The political campaign will need to show what contracts, logistics, law, divided loyalties and constitutional institutions can or cannot do when decency is insufficient.

**Next action:** define the relevant levers for the takeover/exoneration sequence, not a complete invented constitution. Also move the speculative “most likely under the Ministry of the Interior” wording in the public police record to an explicit uncertainty or settle that detail. Preserve the 21st-century state and older constitutional inheritance together.

### 12. Make the parallel Academy and Cardiff operations executable

**Missing operational decisions · high priority.** Natalia sends for help; Lynleit is going to Cardiff; non-Magus Kyrien substitutes at the Academy; Natalia ultimately intervenes. Those dependencies are established. Kyrien's cover, access, objective, communications, extraction limits and the point at which Natalia can intervene are not yet sufficiently concrete to carry a full sequence.

**Next action:** identify one decision or discovery that belongs to Myka rather than to her rescuers, and one problem that requires Kyrien's conventional skills without making the trained Magi foolish. In Cardiff, demonstrate Mikhail's effective method and its cost before asking Lynleit or the reader to judge it. Sources: [Myka](../character.html?character=myka), [Natalia](../character.html?character=natalia), [Mikhail](../character.html?character=father-mikhail), the current Academy/Cardiff Moments and [Questions](../docs.html?doc=questions-to-be-answered).

### 13. Sherie's first acquisition of magical knowledge is still missing

**Knowledge-entry decision · high priority.** Sherie can take part in the six-person debrief in [The Shared Night](../story.html?chapter=the-shared-night), but that establishes only that she knows by then. It does not supply her earlier discovery, who explained the danger, or what she chose to keep from others.

**Next action:** select that earlier event or retain the briefing as unplaced until it is known. Do not bring Hiyu and Yulia into magical briefings to fill a conversational role; they remain outsiders at this stage.

### 14. Lynleit and Kyrien's connected dream remains an author choice

**Acknowledged writer gap · high priority.** The current Shared Night intentionally leaves their private encounter undrafted. The author has ruled out treating it as an established sexual encounter and suggested painful or emotional disclosure. No particular childhood trauma, mutual confession or exact dream image has been selected.

**Next action:** choose what each person experiences, what the other actually learns, and what remains unspoken afterward. Keep their unintended emotional access distinct from Sherie's attempt to avoid that kind of entanglement with Felix. The missing passage must remain an amber writer notice until written; non-canon Anima dream art cannot fill the gap as event evidence.

### 15. The office reprimand needs a scene, not a new outcome

**Dramatization gap · medium priority.** [Fionn's office after the rescue](../moments.html?moment=fionns-office-after-the-rescue) has a strong confirmed reversal: Lynleit invokes Natalia's safety advice; Fionn reveals that Natalia informed him. His objection is recklessness and readiness, not resentment that she rescued someone.

**Next action:** dramatize what Lynleit initially thinks the meeting concerns and the practical consequence of Fionn's judgment. Establish how Natalia learns of the incident if that becomes relevant. The possible irritated exit into a cat chase is not yet a fixed placement for every existing cat panel.

### 16. Sleepers needs placement and an ordinary aftermath

**Author decision · medium priority.** [Sleepers above the River](../moments.html?moment=sleepers-above-the-river) correctly remains unplaced and stops during the journey. The motorboat has been synchronized with the panels. Kyrien's willingness to follow the figures raises useful questions about his current knowledge, permission to use the boat and what he does after the last visible figure.

**Next action:** place the encounter consistently with the River Choir as his entry into magical knowledge, then decide its actual consequence. Do not identify the Sleepers with the Drowned Choir or river leviathan merely because all appear near water. A passive manifestation need not become a combat encounter.

### 17. Hiyu and Yulia need consequences outside their own banter

**Development recommendation · medium priority.** Their complementary curiosity and procedural caution are legible. The park trespass still needs an outcome that affects their ordinary lives or the investigation rather than simply proving that Hiyu is bold and Yulia is sensible.

**Next action:** choose what they discover, what they misunderstand, who notices their absence and what the authorities actually do. A supplied university image showing investigative props does not by itself employ them as police or grant magical clearance. Their own agency should survive any eventual rescue.

### 18. Track the pistol and coat as separate continuity problems

**Equipment continuity · medium priority.** The small silver pistol, spare magazine and final throw in [There Is No Eleventh Floor](../story.html?chapter=there-is-no-eleventh-floor) have clear local uses. The subsequent availability of the Sparrow and Felix's later Rook loan still need a recover/reissue/loan sequence once these scenes are placed. The ordinary suede jacket lost in the river is not the autonomous leviathan-hide coat.

**Next action:** add a brief equipment-state line to the relevant event notes. Do not recover the thrown weapon off-page as an assumed fact, merge the two coats, or identify the coat's source creature with the river creature without an author decision. Owners: [Weapons](../weapons.html), [Items & Artefacts](../items.html), Lynleit's profile and the scene records.

### 19. Sleepers' jacket pockets may conflict with the signature design

**Conditional cross-media conflict · medium priority.** The current prose puts Kyrien's hands in jacket pockets, and the panels show pockets on a light jacket. His fixed signature jacket is pocketless. Characters can own other jackets, so this is not automatically proof of a bad scene.

**Resolve:** identify the Sleepers garment as a different approved outfit, or revise the pocket action/design when its intended identity is confirmed. Do not remove every pocket from Kyrien's wardrobe or modify the male artwork under a female face/hair repair request.

### 20. Blue-flame preparation still disagrees with several images

**Confirmed visual continuity issue · high priority.** The current elevator and river prose put on protective gloves and roll the corresponding sleeves. Older flame artwork and chibis, including IMG-000015, IMG-000018, members of IMG-000078–000083 and river studies IMG-000140–000143, require a separate glove/sleeve check: some show exposed hands or cuffs still down during active flame.

**Resolve:** compare each exact hand and cuff to the depicted moment, then authorize costume/hand edits where needed. The present face/hair revisions deliberately preserve clothing and pose; they must not be described as fixing flame preparation. Water walking without flame does not itself require rolling sleeves. The approved weapon archive remains mandatory where a handled weapon type has a registered reference.

## Prose, relationships and reveal handling

### 21. Complete the two writer gaps without changing the approved boundaries

**Acknowledged missing prose · medium priority.** The Bench under the Lamp and The Shared Night are correctly marked `writer-gap`, not complete scenes. The former needs its missing passage and a credible return to field safety; the latter needs the nonsexual emotional encounter discussed above. A prose percentage or a blue presence bar is not evidence that either passage exists.

**Next action:** work from the preserved author directions. Do not bridge the Shared Night gap with a kiss between Sherie and Felix; the current no-kissing boundary applies inside and outside the dream. Do not move the later park intimacy into the nonsexual hotel refuge or into the connected dream.

### 22. Keep Doom's mechanism and later intimacy distinct

**Continuity safeguard · high priority when expanding.** [Doom Has an Address v7](../story.html?chapter=doom-has-an-address&version=v7) preserves Natalia's ego hypothesis, her practical teaching and Mikhail's specific teasing. The incomplete attempt, reciprocal identity change, later completed intercourse and eventual pregnancy are distinct developments.

**Next action:** when filling the surrounding battle/aftermath, establish what triggers the investigation and what observable change supports the outcome. Do not turn sex into a general cure, ordinary physicians into magecraft advisers, or Mikhail's family joke into a compulsory Cult duty. Keep Natalia's hypothesis from leaking into earlier narrators' certainty.

### 23. Do not normalize every narrator into the same efficient voice

**Editorial recommendation · medium priority.** Kyrien's practical attention to routes, footing, cards and other people's incomplete answers differs from Lynleit's responsibility, preparation, embarrassment and effort to recover authority. The elevator victim has neither character's trained vocabulary. These differences matter more than adding slang or short sentences everywhere.

**Next action:** preserve the asymmetry while revising connective passages. Review repeated explanatory conclusions, especially when a gesture already communicated the point. Keep productive comic repetition in the church and Mikhail exchanges. MBTI may support the review but does not dictate a facial expression, a gender rule or a uniform speaking rhythm.

### 24. The two outlines need different kinds of development

**Development recommendation · medium priority.** The Last Man Out concerns a consequential catastrophe and Heyk's survival; The Name Left Behind is an earlier scene seed. Both are honestly labelled outlines, but they cannot yet carry the same evidentiary weight as complete Chapters.

**Next action:** for the former, establish the rescue/escape chain and the survivor's usable knowledge; for the latter, decide the central event and its actual plot connection before expanding atmosphere. Keep linked Moment fact lists neutral until prose shows something. Do not manufacture a “reader inference” from text that does not exist.

### 25. Let mysteries remain local rather than collapsing them into one explanation

**Worldbuilding safeguard · medium priority.** Ordinary fog predates the Spill; unusual fog has incident-specific evidence. The Drowned Choir, river leviathan, Sleepers, Nameless Street, office-building incident and Bone Archive are not established expressions of one shared intelligence. Anima is outside story canon, and her nickname does not turn her into the in-world explanation of Inanna.

**Next action:** distinguish a repeated image from a confirmed mechanism whenever a new Chapter connects incidents. Give each mystery practical consequences and limits without inventing a universal supernatural taxonomy just to explain the recurrence.

### 26. Historical differences should stay historical

**Audit safeguard.** Earlier dream kisses, older Lynleit ENFJ readings and previous river narration remain accessible in version history. The current dream revision removes the kiss; the current cognition record and Doom v7 notes use the later suspected INFJ interpretation. The older behavior notes are version-scoped. They are not current canon conflicts merely because a text search finds them.

**Next action:** retain clear current/default labels and direct historical links. Update genuinely stale cross-record claims such as findings 01–02, not every historical sentence. Search's earlier-version option must not make old facts appear current by default.

### 27. Publication storage is close to the hosting limit

**Publication boundary corrected; capacity decision remains.** Before publication, tracked files plus the 108 new revisions occupied about 1,199 MiB. Roughly 257 MiB belonged to archived backlog sources, not to registered public content. The Pages configuration now excludes backlog and local development folders while preserving their repository history and every registered public original.

The estimated public source payload is still about 942 MiB, close to [GitHub Pages' 1 GB published-site limit](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits). This estimate is not a measurement of the final server artifact. Future large intakes need a storage plan, such as a separately approved asset host or a lossless delivery-format policy. Do not delete preserved versions to make a build fit. Removing backlog from Pages does not make previously committed sources secret or revoke copies already obtained.

## Artwork: correction standard and version boundaries

Each correction uses the target original, the applicable character identity package or approved artwork, the profile's Height and build and Compare proportions guidance, and the author's broad connected hair-shape examples. For an existing image the original head/body proportions, pose, expression, outfit, framing and scene take precedence over another reference's crop or body. Smooth connected masses and a few purposeful planes replace sprays of thin strands. A pencil picture is not exempt from this requirement, but the rest of its drawing medium remains intact.

- Lynleit's normal loose hair is waist-length, not hip-, seat- or floor-length. Her fancy, ponytail and Arc 2 bun variants are not interchangeable.
- Sherie's high bun contains most of her hair, with a smaller loose section over one shoulder to the elbow or slightly lower. A high bun plus an entire loose back mane fails that design.
- Yulia's chestnut hair and blunt fringe end at the natural elbow, with a restrained connected silhouette.
- Myka's approved identity and stylization remain her own; a Lynleit hair-construction example cannot transfer Lynleit's face or clothing.
- Anima's approved long-haired template raises a specific exception question. Historical author drawings and deliberately inhuman Holumn forms must not be silently converted into ordinary cast templates.

Only visually reviewed corrections are eligible for publication. Original bytes, old image IDs and direct reader links are retained. A new v2 receives its own permanent ID and FULL/PREV filenames. Gallery defaults, profile rotation and panel slideshows select current revisions; older artwork remains available through version controls or a retained-original disclosure. A hair correction does not change the original's Fan Service, H Scene or canon classification.
