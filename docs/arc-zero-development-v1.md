# Arc 0: Households and the Becoming of MSF

## Status and scope

This is a tentative prequel direction, not a fixed chronology or a completed account of MSF's founding. Arc 0 is a separate life period centered on Fionn, his household, and the development of MSF. The younger cast's high-school years provide parallel formation stories beneath that adult narrative. Exact dates, ages, institutional milestones, and school arrangements remain undecided.

The [Arc 0 outline](../moments.html?moment=households-before-the-spill) collects its sparse scene anchor. No separate prequel Chapter is established yet.

## Fionn's story

Fionn needs objectives, friendships, political obligations, weaknesses, and decisions that do not exist solely to explain his daughter. Follow him as a widower, father, professional, political actor, and man able to love again. His remarriage and his work should affect each other through concrete pressures rather than parallel exposition.

MSF should become recognizable through people solving immediate problems. Temporary arrangements, practical necessity, threats, institutional failures, loyalties, and compromises may harden into lasting practices. Do not assign a founding date, invent a precursor organization, or assume that nothing called MSF existed before these years. The distinction between founding and later restructuring needs an author decision.

## Two households

### Lynleit, Fionn, and Helena

Lynleit's mother died while she was still a junior schooler. The move from Turon to Vilen and her third-floor condition retain that existing chronology. Arc 0 can explore the continuing grief during her teenage years without moving the death or relocation into high school.

The proposed domestic arc follows a household rebuilding after loss. Helena's arrival changes meals, seating, schedules, private conversations, rooms, and who can interrupt Fionn. Lynleit may understand that Helena cannot replace her mother while reacting as though a familiar gesture has erased something. Her fear concerns memory as much as possession of her father's attention.

Let Helena genuinely give Fionn companionship. Lynleit then has to recognize a legitimate place for someone she resents. An apology, accepting help, or admitting Helena was right are possible scenes, not established events. Do not rewrite Helena's later actions as inevitable from her first entrance.

Ordinary father-daughter familiarity matters: a late meal, an argument about school, waiting while he works, a manipulation he recognizes immediately, or discovering that he does not know an answer. Not every conversation needs a lesson or a later callback.

### Kyrien and his family

The proposed teenage arc gives Kyrien a mother and sister whom he tries to protect after his father's death. Their names, ages, circumstances, and present-day status remain open. Show their ordinary life before the loss: meals, arguments, unwanted chores, jokes, and someone expecting him home.

His father's death makes lineage a practical burden before he has the resources or authority to carry it. Money, legal standing, inherited danger, dismissive officials, and relatives are candidate pressures, not confirmed incidents. His mother and sister must retain their own judgment and sometimes reject his interference.

The useful conflict is between responsibility and actual capacity. Accepting help, listening, or standing down can cost him more pride than a fight. Institutions may include people acting reasonably who still cannot help his family. Avoid making either solitary defiance or official procedure a universal answer.

## Adolescence and developing habits

Young Lynleit should make mistakes her adult self has learned to hide. Curiosity, political awareness, competitiveness, family privilege, immature manipulation, and visible grief may coexist. Young Kyrien can be easier to read, angrier, more trusting, and quicker to mistake determination for resources. Neither is the finished adult in school clothes.

The proposed contrast is specific: Lynleit learns to keep important people near after loss; Kyrien learns to function when alone. Proximity can support loyalty or become possessiveness. Independence can support survival or make help difficult to accept. These are development directions to test in scenes, not explanations for every adult action.

Kyrien's [whiskey flask](../items.html?item=kyriens-whiskey-flask) is established adult equipment. Arc 0 may explore its beginnings through his father, an older relative, imitation, inheritance, or a difficult night. None of these origins is selected. The habit may begin awkwardly and become a measured private ritual. Do not depict alcohol as medical treatment or as the source of his competence.

## School and the ensemble

Use classmates, family backgrounds, examinations, rivalries, meals, clubs, and ordinary boredom to make the younger ensemble socially alive. Do not assign all current characters to the same school or generation. Kyrien's later introduction to the Magi world remains intact: magical schooling and doctrine belong to characters who already have access to them, not automatically to him.

For young Magi, lessons, exercises, history, doctrine, and unequal access to training can show how their institutions describe themselves. Teachers may be sincere yet transmit omissions or mistaken assumptions. A lesson is evidence of what students were taught, not automatic confirmation of the world's underlying truth. Talent should create expectations and mistakes as well as prestige.

Friendships and rivalries may change without every classmate becoming an important enemy. Warmth, adolescent certainty, humor, family affection, and small victories should coexist with class, political pressure, magical privilege, and threats the adults cannot always contain.

## Optional forgotten encounter

A brief teenage encounter between Lynleit and Kyrien remains optional. A waiting room, station, shop, school event, or corridor could let two exhausted adolescents irritate each other and inadvertently challenge a defensive assumption. No venue, dialogue, or meeting is confirmed.

If used, keep it non-romantic. No secret relationship, destiny framing, promise, or compulsory later recognition follows. It must not give Fionn a meaningful relationship with adult Kyrien or give Kyrien early knowledge of magic. The adult interrogation remains their first proper meeting.

## Ending and retrospective effect

Arc 0 need not run directly into Arc 1. A household equilibrium, departure, graduation, meal, or early MSF assignment could close the formative period. These are alternatives, not an ending montage.

Let later knowledge change the reader's experience of ordinary places and habits. Avoid announcing which object or conversation will matter. Fionn never properly knows the adult Kyrien who becomes important to Lynleit; do not manufacture that missing relationship to comfort the reader. The prequel must not reveal the future child.

## Questions before Chapters

- Which MSF changes occur in these years, and what already exists beforehand?
- When does Fionn remarry relative to the move and Lynleit's school years?
- How old is Kyrien when his father dies, and what happens to his mother and sister?
- Which younger characters overlap in age and schooling?
- Which origin, if any, belongs to the flask?
- Does the forgotten encounter happen at all?

The Nasu audit favors the conflict between useful institutions and the households affected by their decisions. Its restraint here is to keep the candidate incidents optional rather than turn a development discussion into a finished history.
