## Lynleit

### Confirmed: uncertainty and consultation

When Lynleit has not decided what to do, she feels that the answer exists internally but is still obscure. Talking to people helps her bring it into focus faster. Kyrien would notice distress that she is trying to conceal.

A strong objection from someone she trusts surprises and unsettles her even if her plan is working and the objection adds no new facts. She wonders whether she selfishly pushed for something that others were not comfortable with.

Trusted people's confidence can sometimes help her proceed despite an unexplained sense that something is wrong. It does not fully settle her internal commitment. She may postpone a plan without being able to give a complete explanation.

She continues to treat openness as a leadership skill. Concealing distress, or becoming evasive about an embarrassing personal subject, does not turn her general leadership into secrecy. MSF duty and her personal missions remain primary.

### Confirmed: physical competence

Lynleit is somewhat more agile and nimble than the average woman. She is generally competent at keeping herself alive under imminent physical threat. This is not a claim of exceptional strength, limitless stamina, invulnerability, or a magical enhancement of her ordinary movement.

### Working interpretation

INFJ with strong Fe now fits the discussion better than the previous ENFJ with strong Ni label. The important distinction is the persistence of an internally forming interpretation even when others reassure her, not quietness or a dreamy appearance. Her leadership, warmth, initiative, and familiar student-friend behavior with Natalia remain intact.

Possible physical development would emphasize balance, repositioning, and recovery from compromised movement. Specific exercises, teachers, sports, and a training history have not been chosen. The operational role provides an opportunity for training, not proof of a particular curriculum.

## Kyrien

### Confirmed: observation and reasoning

When something behaves inexplicably, Kyrien first looks for noticeable features or patterns that might indicate danger. An explanation that seems logically wrong continues to occupy him even if the associated approach repeatedly works. Several unresolved problems may continue developing at the back of his mind.

An answer can arrive as an overall realization before he has reconstructed its reasoning. Urgent action may follow. When a realization proves wrong, he wants to understand which assumption distorted the picture and how to prevent the same practical failure. The exact steps he takes to investigate that error remain undecided.

His particular usefulness comes from noticing what others miss in both physical surroundings and unfolding situations. He prepares for developments that most people do not yet see coming. When those developments become immediate threats, he can turn preparation into effective tactical action. This makes him valuable to Lynleit when conspiracy or isolation deprives her of her usual support.

This is observation and inference, not foreknowledge or magical perception. It does not require every suspicion to be right.

### Confirmed: physical competence

Kyrien is good at endurance and precision in his movements and actions. His already-established relative strength and ability to negotiate obstacles remain relevant. Which kinds of endurance are strongest, their limits, and the conditioning that supports them are not yet specified.

### Working interpretation

ISTP remains the leading interpretation; INTJ is still an alternative. A Ti-Se-Ni reading can accommodate independent reasoning, concrete observation, and anticipatory insight. An overall realization or good preparation does not by itself settle his dominant function.

Repeatability and economical movement could give his precision a consistent physical expression. Competitiveness and preparedness could motivate sustained practice. Neither proposal establishes a training routine. Childhood familiarity with weapons does not by itself account for adult proficiency.

## Sherie

### Confirmed: social attention

Without diplomatic duties or anyone she needs to impress, Sherie still enjoys social situations as play. She teases, provokes mischief, and looks for possibilities that would make a dull gathering more enjoyable. She generally becomes quiet when unwell or uncomfortable.

When someone unexpectedly stops responding as she anticipated, her attention first goes to what changed emotionally in that person and in the whole situation she believed she understood.

If someone agrees for a mistaken reason and the outcome still benefits her, she may joke that she failed successfully. She also considers it a liability in her ability to influence future outcomes. A useful result does not mean she understands how she produced it.

### Working interpretation and relationship use

ENFJ with strong Fe remains supported. Playfulness alone does not establish Ne dominance; her response to an unexpected turn is particularly attentive to emotional change.

Her enjoyment of social play should remain visible alongside professional skill. Not every tease is a calculated diplomatic maneuver. With Felix, sensitivity to a change in atmosphere can coexist with uncertainty about his attachment to Lynleit. No particular confrontation or recognition scene was established by these answers.

## Felix

### Confirmed: interpretation and reassurance

Felix readily generates multiple options and interpretations. He enjoys doing so even when nobody needs cheering up and nothing important is at stake.

In a threatening or discouraging situation, he is often the first to joke. He keeps trying interpretations until he finds one that feels hopeful or uplifting. He may offer it to help people cope even if he privately thinks it is probably false, while continuing to explore the problem internally.

Evidence and personal significance influence which interpretation he eventually prefers. Immediate reassurance is therefore not always his settled belief.

If someone he respects tells him that his continued attachment to Lynleit is hurting Sherie, his first response would be a stunned "oh": bewilderment, perhaps shock that he did not notice it himself. This establishes a likely response to the hypothetical situation, not an encounter that has already happened or a decision about what he does next.

### Confirmed: physical competence

Felix's training gives him general physical competence as a field officer. His particular technical contribution remains hacking and communications. He need not be an exceptional athlete to be useful, and his humor does not erase acquired skill.

### Working interpretation

ENFP is now favored over ESFP because generating alternative interpretations is a habitual pleasure, not merely a response to immediate danger. Ne is the clearer inference. Evidence and personal significance leave the judging-function question less settled; Fi remains tentative. Reassuring people does not on its own prove Fe dominance.

His attachment to Lynleit still takes time to loosen. Recognizing Sherie's hurt should not instantly resolve it or prescribe an apology, confession, or new relationship milestone.

## Physical talent and personality

Keep aptitude, learned technique, conditioning, experience, and the use of attention distinguishable. MBTI preferences do not measure skill or ability, as the [Myers-Briggs Company's own explanation](https://www.themyersbriggs.com/en-US/Support/MBTI-Facts) states. Here the framework is an editorial vocabulary, not a scientific explanation of reflexes, athleticism, or supernatural power.

The working method discussed is to identify what a talent reliably allows, where it came from, and what can make it fail. These are questions to resolve through accepted scenes and history, not automatic weaknesses to add. Training, injury, fatigue, an unfamiliar situation, or a mistaken reading can matter without making a competent character inept.

Do not infer physical aptitude from an MBTI function position, gender alone, elegance, clothing, or a slender build. An INFJ can be agile; an ENFP can be a trained field officer. Preserve established magecraft separately rather than inventing a supernatural explanation for ordinary physical skill.
