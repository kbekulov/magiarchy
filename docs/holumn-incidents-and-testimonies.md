# Holumn Incidents and Victim Testimonies

## Archive scope

This document collects incidents and first-person testimonies associated with Holumn activity. The same records circulate among several factions, but their interpretations differ sharply. Police and government investigators usually classify them as crimes, contamination, equipment failure, mass panic, or unexplained deaths. Magi and Church special envoys read the same details as signs of a breach in the hidden order.

The archive does not treat every testimony as a complete description of a Holumn. Some records describe only an effect, a trace, or a witness's damaged understanding.

## Testimony 001: The voice on the line

> Have you heard of a telephone call that answers to you in your own voice? At first I thought whoever was on the phone was repeating me. But it was my own voice. At first it felt like an echo, because all it did was repeat my words. Then it started calling me incessantly, and it was talking to me in bizarre ways. In my own voice.

**Recorded classification:** Disembodied will or communications anomaly.

**Ordinary reading:** Fraud, harassment, a compromised telephone line, equipment failure, or a person in distress.

**Magi and envoy reading:** An echo crossed from reflection into agency. The voice may be the residue of a person, a curse attached to the line, or a Holumn that uses imitation as its first form of contact. No single explanation has been established.

## Incident record format

Future entries should record the witness account first, then the ordinary interpretation, the Magi or Church interpretation, the observed effects, and any known weakness or means of escape. Physical creatures, environmental phenomena, events, disembodied wills, and curses all belong in this archive when they produce a documented incident.
