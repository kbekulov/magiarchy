# Character Behaviour Audit

This writer-facing record tests whether a reaction feels specific to the character who performs it. The notes combine established history, physical circumstances, sex, social expectations, and MBTI function emphasis. They are possibilities for the author to accept, reject, or revise. They are not canon until they enter a story record.

## How the audit works

- Begin with what the character already wants, fears, notices, and knows how to do.
- Use sex and social experience where the scene makes them materially relevant: physical leverage, clothing, public danger, expectations learned in childhood, or the way another person reads the character.
- Use MBTI functions to shape attention and decision-making. They do not excuse an action or predetermine it.
- A useful note creates a more precise choice. A broad claim that any man or woman would behave the same way should be discarded.
- Story-pressure notes identify a possible payoff or image. They remain separate from confirmed scene facts.

## The river operation

> ♀ **Lynleit · ENFJ with strong Ni** Lynleit is likely to remain in command while other people are still in immediate danger, then feel the bodily violation of the attack once she reaches the trees. Her fury underwater fits both her childhood fear and her refusal to become helpless before something using human bodies as tools. The later complaint about the lost jacket can be concrete and mildly childish. She liked the garment, chose it for the operation, and had no chance to save it.

> ♂ **Kyrien · ISTP lean** Kyrien is more likely to protect Lynleit by controlling what he says than by offering reassurance. His officer upbringing, private reserve, and Ti-led caution all support a literal answer that cannot be disproved. The restraint should not make him passive. He notices where she is exposed, where the official team has gone, and what his silence will cost if another authority learns the truth.

> ♀ **Maren · type unknown** Maren is a trained officer, not a weaker swimmer. The horror lies in losing ownership of her own movement when hands seize her legs. Once ashore, a short sensory statement is more credible than a theory: she knows what fingers felt like, but does not claim to know who they belonged to. Anger or embarrassment at having needed to be hauled over the wall may surface after the immediate shock.

> ♂ **Reiner · ISTJ with strong Si and Te** Reiner's greater build makes him the practical anchor for Maren and Ivo, but the river prevents strength from solving the whole problem. He is likely to convert fear into tasks: secure the person, reach the wall, count the team, check the line. His later guilt would appear as stricter procedure and repeated equipment checks rather than a broad confession.

> ♂ **Felix · ESFP with strong Se and Fi** Remaining ashore should frustrate Felix because his most valued people descend without him. His role is still decisive. He keeps the cameras frozen, hears the failure before he can see it, abandons the van at the correct moment, and turns a communications fallback into a physical rescue line. A joke after the extraction can be an attempt to restore ordinary human rhythm, not proof that he took the danger lightly.

> ✦ **Story-pressure note** The blue flame can expose the scale of the horror without an explanation. Hands burning in blue beneath the surface would briefly illuminate different ages, sizes, and states of decay before the river closes over them again. The image would let the reader understand that Lynleit is fighting accumulated dead rather than a single submerged attacker.

## The Nameless Street

> ♀ **Lynleit · ENFJ with strong Ni** The crying girl works as a lure because Lynleit notices a vulnerable person and assumes responsibility before she can test the situation. This does not require a general claim that women are more nurturing. Her Fe-oriented leadership, strong symbolic intuition, and experience of being watched make the trap personal. Afterward she may be angrier that the street used her protective instinct than frightened by the child herself.

> ♂ **Felix · ESFP with strong Se and Fi** Felix experiences an ordinary physical emergency while Lynleit experiences the impossible street. His first response is immediate and tactile: get close, make noise, fan her face, check whether she responds. Once she wakes, his familiarity gives him room to tease, but affection should make him notice that her explanation is not a performance. He can believe that something happened without pretending to understand it.

## Relationships under pressure

> ♀ **Helena · ENTJ with strong Te** Helena's grievance against Fionn becomes stronger if it includes the private humiliation of being treated as a wife in public and a security risk in his deepest life. Her response remains character-specific: she turns exclusion into a structural problem to remove. She is not dangerous because resentment makes her emotional. She is dangerous because she can organize resentment into policy, access, and succession.

> ♂ **Fionn · INTJ with strong Ni** Fionn can sincerely believe that silence is a form of protection because his identity as father, husband, director, and Magiarch has trained him to equate love with containment. A masculine officer culture may reinforce the habit of carrying danger without asking family to share it. The flaw is not lack of feeling. It is his confidence that private endurance gives him the right to decide what everyone else may know.

> ♀ **Yulia · ISTJ with strong Si** Yulia's decision to follow Hiyu into the park can contain two competing reactions: procedural disgust at the trespass and a familiar expectation that she will be the one who prevents his idea from killing him. As a young woman beside a reckless male friend, she may resent being pushed into practical responsibility while still choosing it. Her care appears in preparation, correction, and staying present rather than an open admission of attraction.

> ♂ **Hiyu · ENTP with strong Ne** Hiyu's confidence in an elegant possibility may make the physical risk feel secondary until Yulia reacts to it. His eventual attachment should develop through dependence on the person who makes his ideas survivable. He does not need to become conventionally suave when desire appears. Confusion is more consistent if he recognizes that losing Yulia's company would be intolerable before he recognizes the feeling as romantic.

## Long-term inheritance

> ♂ **Kyrien · probable ISTP moving into command** Directing MSF while Lynleit carries the Magiarchate forces Kyrien into a role built around speech, delegation, and institutional patience. His instinct may be to solve pressure alone because that resembles the officer ideal inherited from his father. A believable failure occurs when he withholds a burden that subordinates needed to understand, repeating Fionn's mistake in a different personality.

> ✦ **Story-pressure note** The first time the leviathan-hide coat imitates Lynleit's lost suede jacket could occur when the resemblance is emotionally inconvenient. The form would turn an irritable complaint about clothing into posthumous evidence that Fionn noticed more of her ordinary preferences than his secrecy allowed him to say.

> ✦ **Story-pressure note** The coat works best as protection with judgment rather than passive armour. Its need to be placed over a specific wound or person forces Lynleit to choose what she covers. Its autonomous form can inconvenience her, but the decision to protect remains hers.
