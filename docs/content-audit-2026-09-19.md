# Content Audit: 19 September 2026

Scope: the current/default eight Chapters, 25 Moments, 18 character profiles, Story phases, World records, six weapon records, two Items & Artefacts records, active writer references, and Gallery and Music descriptions. Historical revisions were checked where needed for source and version continuity, not rewritten. This is a content audit, not an interface or code-architecture review.

## Overall judgment

The strongest material puts particular people inside an immediate problem: Felix's newspaper at the end of the Nameless Street, the river team's failed watches and card games, Natalia making Lynleit work through an example, and Mikhail letting a ceremony become a personal provocation. Those scenes already have recognizable voices. Their pauses, repetitions, and ordinary objects deserve protection.

The main weakness is uneven development. The river and Doom are extensively dramatized, while the political conspiracy, the fugitive campaign, Cardiff, and the Academy still depend heavily on summaries. As a result, the archive explains several relationships and institutions more fully than the story has yet demonstrated them. Cutting the developed scenes would not fix that imbalance. The missing operations need their own decisions, setbacks, and consequences when their facts are chosen.

A second weakness is drift between a scene and its summary. An interpretation becomes a rule, a later consequence becomes a witnessed event, or an open placement becomes an asserted sequence. This pass corrects those transitions without inventing the missing answers.

- The river Moment v3 no longer claims Kyrien saw the underwater flame. It describes his evasive answer instead of calling it a flat denial. The girl's head tilt suggests bewilderment without proving the Holumn's governing rule. Lynleit's attention to his breathing is not attributed to an unshown reciprocal thought.

- Lynleit is included among the participants in Fionn's assassination Moment because finding his body and being accused are central to that record.
- River panels explicitly link to Chapter v2 and Moment v3. Their alternative compositions remain artwork of the same beats, not additional events.

### World and reference accuracy

- Profiles distinguish Mikhail's possible treatment from the later successful outcome in Doom. The Chapter remains v7. A Moment wording error now attributes his observation to his interpretation of Lynleit's behavior.
- The Holumn registry no longer calls the puzzle victim's disappearance permanent. A missing person's return remains unknown, not impossible.

- Real firearm comparisons stay in conceptual references rather than implying an in-world 1911/2011 lineage for the Swan or real-product membership for the Rook.

- Advisory behavior notes require author acceptance. Merely displaying a note beside a scene cannot make it canon.
- Questions no longer assume an unconfirmed near miss, retaliation, physical intimacy, or lasting river alteration. The two already-written surveillance anomalies are no longer requested as missing scene details.

## What still needs author decisions or new scenes

1. **The main political chain.** Choose the concrete actions by criminal Magi, foreign Magiarchates, Church actors, and Helena that converge on Fionn. Their different motives are established; their causal sequence is not. No faction was made the sole mastermind.
2. **Cardiff and the Academy.** The parallel dispatches and rescue structure are sound. The threat, local objective, moral choices, and lasting costs still need definition. Myka needs a consequential decision of her own, not only a summary saying she learned enough to graduate first.
3. **The fugitive campaign.** The refuge and reconnection are useful anchors. The operations between them and Helena's defeat need material obstacles and failures before the institutional reversal can feel earned.
4. **Doom's reciprocal change.** Lynleit's embarrassment is vividly dramatized; Kyrien's inner change is less visible. V7 explicitly supplies the cure explanation. A future revision could make his changed understanding more legible through one specific action, but that action and any change to the concluding explanation have not been chosen here.
5. **The early archive outline.** The Name Left Behind still contains temporary material about Myka, an erased record, and Fionn's name beside a future date. It should not quietly become proof for the main conspiracy, Lester, or Bone Archive.
6. **Later family revelations.** Preserve the two-child direction, the son's reader reveal versus Kyrien's knowledge, and the daughter's post-Arc 2 legibility. Exact scenes, timing, and arrangements are still open.
7. **Sherie and Felix.** Their uneven attachment is well specified as a direction. It needs repeated professional encounters, a moment of Sherie's disappointment, and Felix's recognition of her independent significance. No shortcut through successful flirting was added.
8. **Holumn uncertainty.** The river's surface boundary, Sleepers' meaning, Bone Archive access, and the particulars of Lester's contradictory biography need evidence before explanations. Unknown does not mean interchangeable: each new encounter should add a distinct trace, limit, or cost.

The [Questions ledger](../docs.html?doc=questions-to-be-answered) holds the unresolved choices. No new incompatible canon was selected; the [Contradictions ledger](../docs.html?doc=contradictions-to-resolve) records the reconciliations.

## Publication and verification

Revised documents and scene records retain their earlier published versions. The river's original Chapter file is unchanged. Small copy corrections within an unchanged scene are recorded as errata rather than new plot variants. The Home feed links this audit.

Validation covers generated cross-references, current and historical document dependencies, paragraph-note anchors, scene links, search entries, and browser readers. The automated prose review is a prompt for judgment, not a ban on repeated words. Remaining alerts largely concern editorial records describing version history or characters literally remaining in a place.
