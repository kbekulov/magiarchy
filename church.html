The Church is not ignorant of magic. Restricted parts of the institution know enough to negotiate with Magi, define forbidden boundaries, and preserve a public reality in which none of that knowledge officially exists.

Distributed knowledge

The institution does not know as one body

01
PUBLIC CHURCH

Ordinary faith

Most clergy, staff, and worshippers operate inside the same protected ignorance as the wider public.

02
RESTRICTED CIRCLES

Partial awareness

Certain Church staff know magic exists and can receive Magi for negotiations from which ordinary escorts are excluded.

03
INSTITUTIONAL ACTORS

Power behind doctrine

Church authorities participate in the hidden conflict, balancing sanctioned cooperation against suspicion, pressure, and manipulation.

02

Institutional relations

Every relationship is conditional

WITH THE PUBLICProtect ignorance

Magic remains outside ordinary understanding, and Church knowledge is kept narrow.

WITH THE MAGIARCHYSanction and constrain

Cooperation exists, but so do supervision, suspicion, and boundaries that sanctioned Magi cannot safely cross.

WITH THE STATEReveal only what is necessary

Negotiations can occur near public institutions without making their magical subject visible to ordinary officials or officers.

WITH RENEGADE MAGIOppose unsanctioned power

Magic beyond accepted limits threatens both the Church's authority and the secrecy on which the hidden order depends.

03

Accepted boundaries

Not every possible act is permitted

KNOWN EXAMPLE

Walking on water

Lynleit conceals this accidental thaumaturgy because it crosses a boundary the Church would never accept. She practices alone, at night, far from witnesses.
