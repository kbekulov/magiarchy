Distributed knowledge

The institution does not know as one body

01
MAJORITY OF THE CHURCH

Ordinary faith

Most clergy, staff, and worshippers operate inside the same protected ignorance as the wider public. Religious office alone does not grant access to magical knowledge.

02
HEAD OF THE HIERARCHY

Guaranteed awareness

The head of every church is aware of the Magi and the concealed world surrounding them. This is the stable point from which each church controls its response.

03
CONTROLLED OPERATORS

Special envoys

Each church maintains a contingent of operatives who carry the title of special envoy and act in the name of its head.

Envoys of the heads of churches

Special envoys are trained and presented as priests, allowing them to move through religious institutions with a credible public identity. Their primary preparation is operational rather than pastoral.

03

Institutional relations

Every relationship is conditional

WITH THE PUBLICProtect ignorance

Magic remains outside ordinary understanding, and Church knowledge is kept narrow.

WITH THE MAGIARCHYSanction, constrain, and verify

Cross-national church structures face Magiarchates that are kept territorial and separate. Both sides hunt unsanctioned Magi, but the Church often doubts that Magiarchs will act decisively enough.

WITH THE STATEReveal only what is necessary

Negotiations can occur near public institutions without making their magical subject visible to ordinary officials or officers.

WITH RENEGADE MAGIHunt through special envoys

Magi without a jurisdiction who practice outside church supervision or permission are treated as criminals. Special envoys pursue them alongside the resources of the responsible Magiarch.

04

Accepted boundaries

Not every possible act is permitted

FORBIDDEN MIRACLE-MAKING

Harmless effect, prohibited form

The Church distinguishes pressure-based magecraft from miracle-making that resembles wizardry or imitates acts associated with Jesus, saints, saviours, and other sacred figures. Walking on water crosses that symbolic boundary. Lynleit conceals the accidental talent because exposure would bring strong punishment even if she had harmed nobody.

05

Canon doctrine

Suppression doctrine

THE PRACTICAL RULE

Protect the boundary of public knowledge

Suppression doctrine is a shared responsibility of the Church and the Magiarchy for keeping witnesses of magic from carrying dangerous knowledge back into ordinary society. The Church holds final sanction over the doctrine, while Magiarchal resources may investigate, contain, absorb, or remove witnesses under that authority. It is not one fixed punishment. The response depends on what the witness knows, how credible they are, how much exposure has already occurred, and whether the witness can be contained without creating a larger crisis.

01 · IRREVERSIBLE

Elimination

Murder, assassination, or kidnapping may be considered when the Church believes exposure cannot be contained by other means.

02 · PRESSURE

Coercion

Extortion, intimidation, bribery, and threats can redirect a witness without making the magical truth public.

03 · REFRAME

Discrediting

A witness may be framed as mentally ill, unreliable, or otherwise incapable of persuading anyone who hears the account.

04 · ABSORPTION

Church integration

Some witnesses are forced into Church staff or clergy, where their access, movement, and story can be controlled from inside the institution.

05 · SERVICE

Magiarchal integration

Others may be absorbed into Magiarchy service staff or a supervised operational role, turning dangerous knowledge into a controlled obligation under Church-sanctioned authority.

UNRESOLVED BOUNDARY

The doctrine can preserve civilization while also making murder administratively thinkable. The story's open question is where containment ends and institutional killing begins.

06

Story contact

The first negotiation is visible only from outside

Early in September, Fionn and Lynleit enter a church for negotiations while Felix and Reiner remain outside. They are not merely assigned officers: both are Lynleit's friends and personal bodyguards trusted by Fionn. Even they are denied entry. The subject is hidden from them and from the reader, while Lynleit's early, irritated exit suggests that something important happened inside. Father Mikhail is confirmed as a special envoy, although his specialization and authority in this or later Church operations remain open.
