Territory

A state along the eastern Baltic

Narvea reaches from the approaches to St. Petersburg toward the southwestern Baltic frontier near Gdansk.

THE PRESSURE POINTMSF headquarters

Turon

Turon is home to the headquarters of MSF, a fully private intelligence business that sells services to the government while retaining control of its own succession and internal affairs. Its officers give the company enough local force to mount a small rebellion or attempt secession if it ever chose to do so. Officer culture, divided logistics, constitutional loyalty, and a genuine lack of separatist ambition keep that possibility theoretical.

  • MSF headquarters and senior officer corps
  • Operational strength beyond ordinary agency needs
  • Potential leverage over the central government
  • Political anxiety that ultimately depends on personal restraint

No rebellion is openly planned. The possibility simply remains in the backrooms of Narvean political life, where senior officials cannot afford to ignore it.

03

Constitutional balance

Parliament governs. The Crown safeguards.

Parliament chooses and controls the government, passes laws, and authorizes public money. The Duke gives constitutional form to those decisions and intervenes only through narrowly defined reserve powers.

C

Ducal sphere

The Duke

Head of state and constitutional guardian.

  • Formally appoints the Prime Minister who commands parliamentary confidence
  • Promulgates laws already passed by Parliament without rewriting them
  • Represents the state, receives ambassadors, and maintains the Court and Ducal Guard
  • Uses narrow reserve powers only during constitutional emergencies

Democratic sphere

Parliament & Government

Elected authority and the executive accountable to it.

04

Diplomatic tradition

The room where Europe stepped back

Narvea became a trusted meeting ground where opposing European powers could settle their differences before rivalry became open conflict.

NEUTRAL GROUND

Authority meets here before force.

Narvea's influence rests less on commanding Europe than on keeping its rivals in the same room long enough to avoid escalation.

LANGUAGE AS CIVIC PRACTICE

Multilingualism is expected, not exceptional.

People in authority are expected to know several languages, but the custom reaches far beyond diplomatic circles. Narveans often move between languages in ordinary conversation for precision, courtesy, emphasis, or simple habit, even when no negotiation is taking place.

THE CITY WITH MANY NAMES

Bird's Nest is only one of its titles.

Most negotiations between rival nations take place in this city. Every country calls it something different, carrying its own name for the same diplomatic ground. Bird's Nest is the Narvean title, and its unusual sound reflects the city's role as the place where competing powers gather without fully belonging to one another.

Service and precedence

Rank is not the same as office

Narvea maintains its own hierarchy of court and civil ranks. A person's rank controls formal precedence, while their actual work may be diplomatic, judicial, academic, military, or administrative.

06

Cultural character

Layered, regional, distinctly Narvean

Narvean is a shared political identity, not a single ethnicity. Different regions keep their own language, faith, architecture, and historical memory.

CIVIC IDENTITY

The state is the common ground.

Regional, linguistic, religious, urban, and rural communities can all be fully Narvean.

INSTITUTIONS

Old military customs, ducal administration, civic government, and modern Narvean constitutionalism.

FAITH

Catholic and Orthodox traditions coexist across different regions.

PUBLIC LIFE

Baltic merchant culture, old aristocratic ceremony, and modern parliamentary politics.

CENTRAL TENSION

A modern country that never fully discarded inherited hierarchy.

Why it matters to the story

Narvea is stable because old and modern power remain in balance. It is also vulnerable for the same reason. Every crisis can become a dispute over who has the right to act, what must remain hidden, and which institution will bear the consequences.
