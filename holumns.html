Holumn is a category of manifestation, not a species. Fear, memory, fixation, and psychic fracture can press into reality as a creature, a natural phenomenon, a repeating event, a disembodied will, or a curse.

MANIFESTATION LOGIC
Fear does not stay inside the mind that creates it.

Psychic pressure gathers around people, objects, places, and systems until it acquires a condition the world is forced to express.

01
Pressure

Fear, memory, fixation, grief, or collective psychic strain gathers around a person, object, place, or system.

02
Expression

The pressure acquires a form or repeating condition that reflects its origin without explaining it cleanly.

03
Consequence

The manifestation changes what bodies, objects, infrastructure, or landscapes are capable of doing.

04

Countermeasures

The weakness belongs to the rule

NO UNIVERSAL COUNTERMEASURE

Destroying a body may not end the manifestation

A physical form may yield to force, but event-bound and curse-bound Holumns require their condition to be interrupted. Environmental Holumns may be inseparable from the place expressing them.

INCIDENT-BOUND EVIDENCE
Every weakness must answer what the Holumn is doing.

Countermeasures are recorded against individual incidents. No category-wide weakness is confirmed.
