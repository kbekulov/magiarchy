Holumns

Holumn is a category of manifestation, not a biological species. Fear, memory, fixation, trauma, and psychic fracture can press into reality as a creature, a natural phenomenon, a repeating event, a disembodied will, or a curse.

01

Definition

Nature and behavior

CORE DISTINCTION
Some Holumns have bodies. Others persist as events, conditions, or disembodied wills.

A physical creature may be fought as a body, but another Holumn may exist as weather, repetition, a command without a speaker, or a condition attached to an object or place.

01Personal source

Individual fear, memory, fixation, or psychic fracture can determine the manifestation's form.

02Collective source

The pressure of a population or place can also acquire an outward form.

03Material consequence

The result can injure, alter, pursue, remove, or kill even when it has no stable body.

04Singular logic

Each manifestation follows the specific wound or pressure from which it emerged.

The Drowned Choir

A pale girl rises from the river with her eyes sewn shut by fishing line and a dress made from dead fish. She enslaves the souls of people drowned in the river and uses their hands to pull new victims beneath the surface.

Lynleit's blue flame burns the submerged hands. Once the surface supports her, the hands beneath her cannot reach through it. This is an observed boundary around her escape, not a proven restriction across the entire river. No method of freeing the enslaved dead or defeating the girl is confirmed.

The River That Is Too Deep

Something immense moves beneath the city river despite stretches too shallow to contain it. Impossible waves damage boats and kayaks, while drowned bodies lose pieces to bites larger than any expected river predator could make.

The Nameless Street

A lost visitor may enter a deserted old street whose lanes, stairs, courtyards, and hills cannot be reconciled with the surrounding city. Following anyone encountered there may prevent the visitor from returning to ordinary streets.

The urban warning instructs visitors not to follow anyone encountered on the street. It is not known whether refusing the lure guarantees escape or why Lynleit returns after following it.

Doom Has an Address

A Holumn transfers its own terminal condition to whoever consciously destroys it. When Lynleit and Kyrien kill it together, one fatal curse identifies both egos as a single causal answer and remains distributed across them.

The curse requires exact sympathetic recognition of the ego or egos responsible for the Holumn's destruction. Biological death breaks that identity absolutely, but profound reciprocal transformation can introduce enough uncertainty to fracture the attachment without killing either subject.

06

Countermeasures

Limits and countermeasures

NO UNIVERSAL RESPONSE

Responses depend on the manifestation

Ordinary weapons may affect some physical bodies, but they cannot solve a manifestation whose essential rule is environmental, conditional, or disembodied.

  1. 01
    Identify the rule

    The visible threat may be only one effect of the governing rule.

  2. 02
    Find its boundary

    Trigger, place, object, memory, or repeated action may define what the Holumn can do.

  3. 03
    Match the intervention

    Some Holumns can be harmed physically. Others require magecraft capable of acting on the condition itself.

BLUE FLAME

Fionn and Lynleit's inherited blue flame becomes indispensable against certain immortal, rapidly regenerating, or nonliving Holumns, and against barriers that ordinary weapons cannot breach. It is a specialized answer, not a universal one.

08

Category exception

Lester carries the boundary inside himself

DEMIHOLUMN

A person who is also part Holumn

Lester retains human agency and acts as Natalia's trusted ally. His part-Holumn nature sometimes permits interventions that ordinary investigation and conventional magecraft cannot achieve. His chronology cannot be reconciled into a continuous causal biography. Neither he nor Natalia knows his origin; it lies beyond the horizon of human singularity and ordinary human comprehension. Natalia recognizes contradiction as intrinsic to nature, without reducing Lester to an explainable transformation or an error in reality.

09

Victims and continuity

Being taken and returning

NO COMMON DESTINATION
Being taken means entering a state imposed by a particular Holumn.

The effect need not involve travel. Location, chronology, physical continuity, identity, life, death, and causality may cease to function normally.

A victim can return. What makes return possible, and whether the same person returns, depend on the Holumn responsible.

Read the archive boundaries and evidence →
01Imposed states

Death is one possible result. Others include madness, contradiction, disrupted time, altered reality, compromised physicality, and damaged or transformed memory.

02Life and non-life

Holumn activity can resemble life after death or reanimate a corpse. Reanimation does not necessarily restore the original person or constitute ordinary resurrection.

03Personal continuity

Reappearance alone does not establish unchanged identity. Each Holumn determines the implications for memory, continuity, and personhood.

04A conceptual analogy

Jungian synchronicity suggests one possibility: meaningful coincidence could become an inhabitable or imprisoning condition rather than an observer's experience. This is an analogy, not a recorded manifestation.
