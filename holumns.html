World/Holumns

Supernatural taxonomy

Holumns

Holumns are manifestations produced when psychic instability, memory, fear, and environmental pressure cross into reality. They are not a single species or fixed bestiary.

Forms

What a Holumn can be

Creatures and bodies

Some Holumns move through the world as animal-like or human-like forms. A body may be alive, dead, or only imitating life.

Weather and nature

Storms, water, forests, and other natural systems can become the visible shape of a pressure that has acquired intention.

Events and curses

A repeated incident, a place-bound rule, or a curse may be the Holumn itself. Not every manifestation has a body to destroy.

Disembodied wills

A memory, voice, or desire can persist as an active presence after it has separated from the person who first carried it.

02

The Spill

Pressure made visible

The Spill is the large-scale crisis in which Holumn activity becomes impossible to treat as isolated incidents. Its first cause and full extent remain unknown, while the disappearances that follow are caused by Holumns.

03

Limits and weaknesses

No universal weakness exists

Each Holumn is shaped by the fear, memory, or pressure that formed it. Its weakness therefore belongs to its origin and behavior. Conventional force may work against a physical body, but it cannot solve a curse, weather phenomenon, or disembodied will by default.

Observed weaknesses are recorded alongside their originating incidents in the Holumn incident and testimony archive. New testimonies refine this reference rather than creating a separate taxonomy.
