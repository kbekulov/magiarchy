World/Holumns

Supernatural manifestation archive

Not every monster
has a body.

Holumn is a category of manifestation, not a species. Fear, memory, fixation, and psychic fracture can press into reality as a creature, a natural phenomenon, a repeating event, a disembodied will, or a curse.

Core rules

What remains true across manifestations

    01

    Taxonomy

    Forms observed so far

    The categories overlap. A manifestation may use a body while also belonging to a river, an object, or a repeated event.

    Origin and effect

    Pressure becomes a rule

    01Pressure

    Fear, memory, fixation, grief, or collective psychic strain gathers around a person, object, place, or system.

    →
    02Expression

    The pressure acquires a form or repeating condition that reflects its origin without explaining it cleanly.

    →
    03Consequence

    The manifestation changes what bodies, objects, infrastructure, or landscapes are capable of doing.

    Weaknesses

    The countermeasure must answer the rule

    A physical body may yield to force, but destroying a visible form does not always resolve the manifestation. Event-bound and curse-bound Holumns require their condition to be interrupted. Environmental Holumns may be inseparable from the place expressing them. Each confirmed weakness therefore belongs to an incident, not to the category as a whole.
